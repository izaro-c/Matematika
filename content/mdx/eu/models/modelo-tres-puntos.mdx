<Definicion title="Ereduaren egitura formala">
Eredua $\mathcal{M} = (\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \mathbf{I})$ tupla gisa definitzen da, <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-disjunto">multzo disjuntu</ConceptLink> eta finituez osatua ($\mathcal{P} \cap \mathcal{L} = \emptyset$):

- **<ConceptLink targetId="punto">Puntuen</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domeinua</ConceptLink>**: $\mathcal{P} = \{$ <VisualBind element="A" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="B" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="C" color="terracota">$C$</VisualBind> $\}$ non $A \neq B \land B \neq C \land A \neq C$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="recta">Zuzenen</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domeinua</ConceptLink>**: $\mathcal{L} = \{$ <VisualBind element="rAB" color="carbon">$\ell_{AB}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="rBC" color="carbon">$\ell_{BC}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="rCA" color="carbon">$\ell_{CA}$</VisualBind> $\}$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">Intzidentzia</ConceptLink>-<ConceptLink targetId="relacion">erlazioa</ConceptLink>** $\mathbf{I} \subseteq \mathcal{P} \times \mathcal{L}$:
  $$
  \mathbf{I} = \{ (A, \ell_{AB}), (B, \ell_{AB}), (B, \ell_{BC}), (C, \ell_{BC}), (C, \ell_{CA}), (A, \ell_{CA}) \}
  $$
  edo baliokideki, zuzen bakoitzaren <ConceptLink targetId="traza">puntu-trazak</ConceptLink> puntuen <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto">multzoan</ConceptLink> identifikatuz: $\operatorname{tr}(\ell_{AB}) = \{A, B\}$, $\operatorname{tr}(\ell_{BC}) = \{B, C\}$ eta $\operatorname{tr}(\ell_{CA}) = \{C, A\}$.
</Definicion>
