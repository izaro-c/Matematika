  <ConceptLink targetId="colinealidad">Kolinealak</ConceptLink> ez diren edozein hiru <ConceptLink targetId="punto">puntutarako</ConceptLink> (<VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="ocre">$B$</VisualBind> eta <VisualBind element="pC" color="ocre">$C$</VisualBind>), gutxienez <VisualBind element="polyTriangulo" color="canela">$\pi$</VisualBind> <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plano</ConceptLink> bat existitzen da, hiru puntuek $\pi$ planoarekin <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">intziditzen</ConceptLink> dutela. Gainera, edozein $\pi$ planotarako, $\pi$ planoarekin intziditzen duen $P$ puntu bat existitzen da:
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  &\forall A, B, C \in \mathcal{P} \ \bigl(\neg \exists \ell \in \mathcal{L} \, (A \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land C \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell) \implies \exists \pi \in \Pi \ (A \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi \land C \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi)\bigr) \\
  &\land \ \forall \pi \in \Pi \ \exists P \in \mathcal{P} \ (P \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi)
  \end{aligned}
  $$
