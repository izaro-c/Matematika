<Definicion title="I.7 axioma (Planoen ebakidura)">
Izan bitez <VisualBind element="plano-pi" color="azul">$\pi_1$</VisualBind> eta <VisualBind element="plano-sigma" color="ocre">$\pi_2$</VisualBind> bi <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plano</ConceptLink> desberdin. Biek <VisualBind element="pA" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind> <ConceptLink targetId="punto">puntu</ConceptLink> batekin <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">intziditzen</ConceptLink> badute, gutxienez $A$-ren desberdina den bigarren <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> puntu bat existitzen da bi planoekin intziditzen duena:

$$
\forall \pi_1, \pi_2 \in \Pi, \, \forall A \in \mathcal{P} \ \Biggl(\Bigl(\pi_1 \neq \pi_2 \land A \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_1 \land A \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_2\Bigr) \implies \exists B \in \mathcal{P} \ \Bigl(B \neq A \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_1 \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_2\Bigr)\Biggr)
$$
</Definicion>
