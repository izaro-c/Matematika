<Definicion title="II.4 axioma (Paschen axioma)">
Izan bitez $\pi \in \Pi$ <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plano</ConceptLink> batean <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">intziditzen</ConceptLink> duten $\mathcal{P}$-ko hiru <ConceptLink targetId="punto">puntu</ConceptLink> ez-<ConceptLink targetId="colinealidad">kolineal</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="ocre">$B$</VisualBind> eta <VisualBind element="pC" color="ocre">$C$</VisualBind>, eta izan bedi plano bereko <VisualBind element="lineL" color="mora">$\ell$</VisualBind> <ConceptLink targetId="recta">zuzen</ConceptLink> koplanar bat ($\operatorname{tr}(\ell) \subseteq \operatorname{tr}(\pi)$), <ConceptLink targetId="vertice">erpinekin</ConceptLink> intziditzen ez duena ($\neg(A \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell)$, $\neg(B \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell)$, $\neg(C \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell)$). Baldin eta $\ell$ zuzenak <VisualBind element="triangle" color="ocre">$\triangle ABC$</VisualBind> triangeluaren <VisualBind element="segAB" color="ocre">$\operatorname{seg}(AB)$</VisualBind> zuzenki irekiko <VisualBind element="pP" color="terracota">$P \in \mathcal{P}$</VisualBind> puntu batekin intziditzen badu non $A * P * B$ den, orduan $\ell$ zuzenak $A * Q * C$ edo $B * Q * C$ betetzen duen <VisualBind element="pQ" color="terracota">$Q \in \mathcal{P}$</VisualBind> puntu batekin ere intziditzen du:

$$
\forall A, B, C \in \mathcal{P} \ \forall \ell \in \mathcal{L} \ \forall \pi \in \Pi \ \Biggl( \Bigl(\neg\exists m \in \mathcal{L} \, (A \, \mathbf{I} \, m \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, m \land C \, \mathbf{I} \, m) \land A \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi \land C \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi \land \operatorname{tr}(\ell) \subseteq \operatorname{tr}(\pi) \land \neg(A \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell) \land \neg(B \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell) \land \neg(C \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell)\Bigr) \implies \Bigl(\exists P \in \mathcal{P} \, (P \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land A * P * B) \implies \exists Q \in \mathcal{P} \, (Q \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land (A * Q * C \lor B * Q * C))\Bigr)\Biggr)
$$
</Definicion>
