### Frogapena urratsez urrats

<ProofStep number={1} target="triangulo" title="Hasierako triangelua">
Bedi <VisualBind element="triangulo" color="carbon">$\triangle ABC$</VisualBind> triangelua, <VisualBind element="angulo-a" color="terracota">$\alpha$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="angulo-b" color="pavo">$\beta$</VisualBind> eta <VisualBind element="angulo-c" color="ocre">$\gamma$</VisualBind> barne-angeluekin.
</ProofStep>

<ProofStep number={2} target="paralela" title="Zuzen paraleloaren eraikuntza">
<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides" isDependency={true}>Paraleloen axioma euklidestarraren</ConceptLink> bidez, <VisualBind element="vertice-c" color="carbon">$C$</VisualBind> <ConceptLink targetId="vertice">erpinetik</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="base-ab" color="carbon">$AB$</VisualBind> oinarriarekiko <VisualBind element="paralela" color="carbon"><ConceptLink targetId="paralelismo">zuzen paralelo</ConceptLink></VisualBind> bakarra marrazten dugu.
</ProofStep>

<ProofStep number={3} target={["alterno-a", "transversal-ac", "angulo-a"]} title="Lehen barne-angelu alterno bikotea">
$AC$ zuzena paraleloak ebakitzen dituen <VisualBind element="transversal-ac" color="terracota"><ConceptLink targetId="transversal">transbertsal gisa</ConceptLink></VisualBind> hartuz, <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-angulos-alternos-internos" isDependency={true}>barne-angelu alternoen teoremaren</ConceptLink> bidez <VisualBind element="angulo-a" color="terracota">$\alpha$</VisualBind> barne-angelua eta $C$-ko <VisualBind element="alterno-a" color="terracota">$\alpha'$</VisualBind> <ConceptLink targetId="angulos-alternos-internos">alternoa</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">kongruenteak</ConceptLink> direla ondorioztatzen da:

<Formula>
  $$ \alpha \cong \alpha' $$
</Formula>
</ProofStep>

<ProofStep number={4} target={["alterno-b", "transversal-bc", "angulo-b"]} title="Bigarren barne-angelu alterno bikotea">
Modu berean, $BC$ zuzena <VisualBind element="transversal-bc" color="pavo">transbertsal gisa</VisualBind> hartuz, <VisualBind element="angulo-b" color="pavo">$\beta$</VisualBind> barne-angelua eta $C$-ko <VisualBind element="alterno-b" color="pavo">$\beta'$</VisualBind> alternoa kongruenteak dira:

<Formula>
  $$ \beta \cong \beta' $$
</Formula>
</ProofStep>

<ProofStep number={5} target={["angulo-llano", "angulo-c", "alterno-a", "alterno-b"]} title="Angelu lautuaren osaera eta ondorioa">
<VisualBind element="alterno-a" color="terracota">$\alpha'$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="angulo-c" color="ocre">$\gamma$</VisualBind> eta <VisualBind element="alterno-b" color="pavo">$\beta'$</VisualBind> angeluak <ConceptLink targetId="angulos-adyacentes">albokoak</ConceptLink> dira $C$-tik igarotzen den zuzen paraleloaren gainean, <VisualBind element="angulo-llano" color="carbon"><ConceptLink targetId="angulo-llano">angelu lautua</ConceptLink></VisualBind> osatuz:

<Formula>
  $$ \alpha' + \gamma + \beta' = 180^\circ $$
</Formula>

$\alpha'$ $\alpha$-gatik eta $\beta'$ $\beta$-gatik ordezkatuz:

<Formula>
  $$ \alpha + \beta + \gamma = 180^\circ $$
</Formula>
</ProofStep>
