    <Clasificador.ErrorComun titulo="Not recognizing the 3, 4, 5 triple or the obtuse angle">
      The triangle with <ConceptLink targetId="lado">sides</ConceptLink> 3, 4 and 5 does not give its angles, but its <ConceptLink targetId="longitud">lengths</ConceptLink> satisfy $3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2$; by the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-pitagoras-reciproco" isDependency={true}>converse of the Pythagorean theorem</ConceptLink>, the angle opposite the side of length 5 is right.     </Clasificador.ErrorComun>

      - **Obtuse**: they have an angle of more than $90^\circ$ ($120^\circ, 30^\circ, 30^\circ$).
    </Clasificador.Resolucion>
  </Clasificador>
