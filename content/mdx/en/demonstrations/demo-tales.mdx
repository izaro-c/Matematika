<Capitular letra="G" />iven a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink> $\triangle ABC$ intersected by a line parallel to one of its sides, <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-tales">Thales's theorem</ConceptLink> is proven. The proof shows that the ratio of segment lengths on one side equals the ratio of corresponding segment lengths on the other side.

<Separador />

### Step-by-step proof

<ProofStep number={1} target="step1" title="Configuration of the triangle and parallel line">
Consider triangle $\triangle ABC$ and a line parallel to side $\overline{BC}$ intersecting sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ at points $D$ and $E$ respectively, forming parallel segment $\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$.
</ProofStep>

<ProofStep number={2} target="step2" title="Equality of areas between parallel lines">
Draw auxiliary segments $\overline{BE}$ and $\overline{CD}$ to form triangles $\triangle BDE$ and $\triangle CDE$. Both triangles share base $\overline{DE}$ and lie between parallel lines $\overline{DE}$ and $\overline{BC}$. Sharing equal altitude between parallels, the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-area-triangulo" isDependency={true}>area of a triangle theorem</ConceptLink> guarantees equality of their areas:

<Formula>
  $$ \text{Area}(\triangle BDE) = \text{Area}(\triangle CDE) $$
</Formula>
</ProofStep>

<ProofStep number={3} target="step3" title="Area ratios and proportionality conclusion">
Comparing these triangle areas with the upper triangle $\triangle ADE$:
1. Triangles $\triangle ADE$ and $\triangle BDE$ share altitude with respect to line $\overline{AB}$, so their area ratio equals the ratio of their bases:
$$ \frac{\text{Area}(\triangle ADE)}{\text{Area}(\triangle BDE)} = \frac{|\overline{AD}|}{|\overline{DB}|} $$
2. Similarly, $\triangle ADE$ and $\triangle CDE$ share altitude with respect to line $\overline{AC}$:
$$ \frac{\text{Area}(\triangle ADE)}{\text{Area}(\triangle CDE)} = \frac{|\overline{AE}|}{|\overline{EC}|} $$

Because denominators are equal by the previous step, the ratio equality follows:

<Formula>
  $$ \frac{|\overline{AD}|}{|\overline{DB}|} = \frac{|\overline{AE}|}{|\overline{EC}|} $$
</Formula>
</ProofStep>
