<Capitular letra="T" />he third congruence axiom of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> introduces the additive property for concatenations of line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink>. If two pairs of adjacent segments along two lines are pairwise congruent, then the total segments formed by their union are also <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruent</ConceptLink> to each other.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  Let $A, B, C$ be three <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> on a line with $A\text{-}B\text{-}C$, and let $D, E, F$ be three points on another line with $D\text{-}E\text{-}F$. If $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{DE}$ and $\overline{BC} \cong \overline{EF}$, then $\overline{AC} \cong \overline{DF}$:
  $$
  (A\text{-}B\text{-}C \land D\text{-}E\text{-}F \land \overline{AB} \cong \overline{DE} \land \overline{BC} \cong \overline{EF}) \implies \overline{AC} \cong \overline{DF}
  $$
</Definicion>

### Visual representation and interaction

In the interactive simulation, composite segments <VisualBind element="segAC" color="terracota">$\overline{AC}$</VisualBind> are constructed from components <VisualBind element="segAB" color="terracota">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="segBC" color="terracota">$\overline{BC}$</VisualBind>. Modifying the length of either summand dynamically preserves the total linear congruence of the combined sum.

### Geometric significance and scope

This axiom underpins the algebra of lengths in synthetic geometry. It enables defining the operation of addition on congruence classes $[\overline{AB}] + [\overline{BC}] = [\overline{AC}]$, endowing lengths with the algebraic structure of an ordered commutative semigroup in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Compatibility with order:** Geometric addition strictly preserves linear ordering ($\overline{AB} < \overline{CD} \implies \overline{AB} + \overline{EF} < \overline{CD} + \overline{EF}$).
- **Strict monotonicity:** The length of the combined segment is strictly greater than each of its constituent parts.
- **Basis of subtraction:** Establishes the geometric difference of segments when one is properly contained within another.
