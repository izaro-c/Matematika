<Capitular letra="I" />f two pairs of adjacent <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>segments</ConceptLink>, situated on the same <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> or on distinct lines, have their component segments respectively <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruent</ConceptLink>, then the composite segments resulting from their concatenation are also congruent to each other.

This postulate links the <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>betweenness</ConceptLink> relation with congruence, ensuring the <ConceptLink targetId="adicion">additivity</ConceptLink> of synthetic <ConceptLink targetId="longitud">lengths</ConceptLink> and formalizing <ConceptLink targetId="suma-segmentos">segment addition</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Axiom III.3 (Segment Addition)">
Let <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="ocre">$B$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pC" color="terracota">$C$</VisualBind> be three <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> on a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> in the primitive <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domain</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$ such that $B$ is <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre">between</ConceptLink> $A$ and $C$ ($A * B * C$), and let <VisualBind element="pD" color="ocre">$D$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pE" color="ocre">$E$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pF" color="terracota">$F$</VisualBind> $\in \mathcal{P}$ be three points on the same or another line such that $E$ is between $D$ and $F$ ($D * E * F$). If <VisualBind element="ABDE" color="ocre">$\overline{AB} \cong \overline{DE}$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="BCEF" color="terracota">$\overline{BC} \cong \overline{EF}$</VisualBind>, then the <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="segAC" color="pavo">$\overline{AC}$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="segDF" color="pavo">$\overline{DF}$</VisualBind> are <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruent</ConceptLink> to each other (<VisualBind element="ACDF" color="pavo">$\overline{AC} \cong \overline{DF}$</VisualBind>):

$$
\forall A, B, C, D, E, F \in \mathcal{P} \ \Biggl( (A * B * C \land D * E * F) \implies \Bigl( (\overline{AB} \cong \overline{DE} \land \overline{BC} \cong \overline{EF}) \implies \overline{AC} \cong \overline{DF} \Bigr) \Biggr)
$$
</Definicion>
