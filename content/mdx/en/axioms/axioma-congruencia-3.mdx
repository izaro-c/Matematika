<Capitular letra="T" />he third <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> axiom of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">Hilbert</ConceptLink> formalizes the additivity of segment layout along a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink>. It asserts that concatenating congruent <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> preserves the overall congruence of the combined sum in <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-absoluta">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="">
  Let $A, B, C$ be three <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> on a line such that $B$ is <ConceptLink targetId="orden">between</ConceptLink> $A$ and $C$, and let $A', B', C'$ be three points on another line such that $B'$ is between $A'$ and $C'$. If $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{A'B'}$ and $\overline{BC} \cong \overline{B'C'}$, then the composite sum segment $\overline{AC}$ is <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruent</ConceptLink> to $\overline{A'C'}$:
  $$
  (A\text{-}B\text{-}C) \land (A'\text{-}B'\text{-}C') \land (\overline{AB} \cong \overline{A'B'}) \land (\overline{BC} \cong \overline{B'C'}) \implies \overline{AC} \cong \overline{A'C'}
  $$
</Definicion>

### Visual representation

Juxtaposing the initial segment <VisualBind element="AB" color="ocre">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind> with the adjacent segment <VisualBind element="BC" color="pavo">$\overline{BC}$</VisualBind> along the given line yields a sum segment <VisualBind element="AC" color="terracota">$\overline{AC}$</VisualBind> that maintains exact rigid correspondence with the concatenation of their transformed congruent parts.

### Axiomatic significance

This postulate permits the synthetic definition of addition on equivalence classes of <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink>. Together with the axioms of <ConceptLink targetId="orden">order</ConceptLink>, it grounds the additive metric structure of geometric space independently of real numbers.
