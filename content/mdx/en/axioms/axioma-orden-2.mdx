<Capitular letra="G" />iven any two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink>, there always exists at least a third point such that the second lies <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> the first and this new point.

This axiom guarantees that no <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> has an endpoint: every <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segment</ConceptLink> $\overline{AB}$ can be extended beyond $B$ and, applying the axiom to the pair $(B, A)$, also beyond $A$.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
For any pair of distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> in the primitive <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domain</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$, there exists at least one point <VisualBind element="pC" color="ocre">$C$</VisualBind> $\in \mathcal{P}$ such that <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> lies between <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pC" color="ocre">$C$</VisualBind>:

$$
\forall A, B \in \mathcal{P} \ \bigl(A \neq B \implies \exists C \in \mathcal{P} \ (A * B * C)\bigr)
$$
</Definicion>

<Nota>
The infiniteness of the <ConceptLink targetId="traza">point trace</ConceptLink> $\operatorname{tr}(\ell)$ of each line does not follow from this axiom and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-1">Axiom II.1</ConceptLink> alone. In the <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-finita">finite geometry</ConceptLink> of the <ConceptLink targetId="modelo-fano">Fano plane</ConceptLink>, whose lines have three points, it suffices to declare that each point of a line lies between the other two in order to satisfy I.1–I.3, II.1, and II.2. <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">Hilbert</ConceptLink> proves that between any two points there are infinitely many points, and hence that $\operatorname{tr}(\ell)$ is an <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-infinito">infinite set</ConceptLink>, by also using <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-3">Axiom II.3</ConceptLink> and Pasch's <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-4">Axiom II.4</ConceptLink>.
</Nota>
