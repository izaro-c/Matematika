<Capitular letra="G" />iven any two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink>, there always exists at least a third point such that the second lies <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> the first and this new point.

Within the framework of <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink> and the foundations established by <ConceptLink targetId="david-hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink>, this postulate guarantees that <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>lines</ConceptLink> have no endpoints or boundary limits, allowing the unbounded extension of any pair of ordered points in <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-hilbertiana">Hilbertian geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Axiom II.2 (Extension of the Line)">
For any pair of distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> in the primitive <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domain</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$, there exists at least one point <VisualBind element="pC" color="ocre">$C$</VisualBind> $\in \mathcal{P}$ such that <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> lies between <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pC" color="ocre">$C$</VisualBind>:

$$
\forall A, B \in \mathcal{P} \ \bigl(A \neq B \implies \exists C \in \mathcal{P} \ (A * B * C)\bigr)
$$
</Definicion>

<Nota>
This axiom ensures the open and boundary-free nature of the line in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>. Together with <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-1">Axiom II.1</ConceptLink> and the axioms of <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incidence</ConceptLink>, it guarantees that the <ConceptLink targetId="traza">point trace</ConceptLink> $\operatorname{tr}(\ell)$ of every line is an <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-infinito">infinite set</ConceptLink> of points, precluding the existence of <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-finita">finite geometries</ConceptLink> endowed with a <ConceptLink targetId="orden-lineal">linear order</ConceptLink> relation.
</Nota>
