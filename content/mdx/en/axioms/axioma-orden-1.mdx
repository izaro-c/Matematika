<Capitular letra="T" />he first order axiom formulated by <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> establishes the symmetric nature and intrinsic collinearity of the ternary <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>betweenness</ConceptLink> relation. If a <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>point</ConceptLink> $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$, all three points belong to the same <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> and are mutually distinct.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  If a <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>point</ConceptLink> $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$ (denoted $A\text{-}B\text{-}C$), then $A, B, C$ are three distinct points on a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> $l \in \mathcal{L}$, and $B$ also lies between $C$ and $A$ ($C\text{-}B\text{-}A$):
  $$
  A\text{-}B\text{-}C \implies \bigl(A \neq B \land B \neq C \land A \neq C \land \exists l \in \mathcal{L} \ (A,B,C \in l) \land C\text{-}B\text{-}A\bigr)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Visual representation and interaction

In the interactive simulation, points <VisualBind element="pA" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="carbon">$B$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="pC" color="terracota">$C$</VisualBind> are arranged linearly along line <VisualBind element="lineDC" color="carbon">$l$</VisualBind>. Central element $B$ satisfies $A\text{-}B\text{-}C$, and reversing geometric reading direction from right to left confirms the symmetric equivalence $C\text{-}B\text{-}A$.

<Separador />

### Geometric significance and scope

Formalizes the primitive relation of one-dimensional ordering without relying on analytical constructs such as real coordinates or numerical metrics. It ensures spatial orientation independence in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Intrinsic symmetry:** The relation $A\text{-}B\text{-}C$ is strictly equivalent to $C\text{-}B\text{-}A$.
- **Mandatory collinearity:** Betweenness is only defined among triads belonging to the same supporting line.
- **Triple distinction:** Excludes coincidences $A = B$, $B = C$, or $A = C$ under the betweenness relation.
