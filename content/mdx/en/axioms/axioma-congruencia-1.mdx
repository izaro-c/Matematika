<Capitular letra="G" />iven any <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>line segment</ConceptLink> and a <ConceptLink targetId="semirrecta" isDependency={true}>ray</ConceptLink> with a specified <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>origin</ConceptLink>, there exists exactly one <ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink> on that ray that forms a segment <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruent</ConceptLink> to the original one.

In the foundation of <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink> by <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink>, this postulate formalizes the unique transport of lengths along any direction in <ConceptLink targetId="espacio">space</ConceptLink>, without appealing to numerical metric scales or coordinate systems.

<Definicion title="Axiom III.1 (Segment Transport)">
Given two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="ocre">$B$</VisualBind> in the primitive <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domain</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$ determining the <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segment</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="segAB" color="ocre">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind>, and a <ConceptLink targetId="semirrecta">ray</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="rayCDir" color="pavo">$r = \overrightarrow{C\,\text{dir}}$</VisualBind> with origin at <VisualBind element="pC" color="terracota">$C$</VisualBind> $\in \mathcal{P}$ directed toward the point <VisualBind element="pDir" color="pavo">$\text{dir}$</VisualBind> $\in \mathcal{P} \setminus \{C\}$, there exists a unique point <VisualBind element="pD" color="terracota">$D$</VisualBind> on the ray $r \setminus \{C\}$ such that the segment <VisualBind element="segCD" color="terracota">$\overline{CD}$</VisualBind> is <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruent</ConceptLink> to $\overline{AB}$:

$$
\forall A, B \in \mathcal{P} \ \Biggl( A \neq B \implies \forall C, \text{dir} \in \mathcal{P} \ \Bigl( C \neq \text{dir} \implies \exists! D \in \operatorname{tr}(\overrightarrow{C\,\text{dir}}) \setminus \{C\} \ (\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}) \Bigr) \Biggr)
$$

Furthermore, every segment is congruent to itself: $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{BA}$.
</Definicion>
