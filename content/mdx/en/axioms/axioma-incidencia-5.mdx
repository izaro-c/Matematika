<Capitular letra="I" />n every <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink> of <ConceptLink targetId="espacio">space</ConceptLink>, the fifth incidence axiom formulated by <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> guarantees the existence of at least three non-collinear <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink>. This postulate ensures that no plane degenerates or collapses into a single straight <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> or a discrete point set.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  For every <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink> $\pi \in \mathcal{E}$, there exist at least three <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> $A, B, C \in \pi$ such that no <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> $l \in \mathcal{L}$ contains all three simultaneously:
  $$
  \forall \pi \in \mathcal{E}, \ \exists A, B, C \in \pi \ \forall l \in \mathcal{L} \ \neg\bigl(A \in l \land B \in l \land C \in l\bigr)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Geometric significance and scope

Guarantees the internal two-dimensionality of each individual plane. It ensures that every planar surface exhibits genuine spatial extent capable of hosting geometric configurations, <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink>, and half-plane separations.

### Properties

- **Non-degeneracy of planes:** Prohibits one-dimensional or point-like planes.
- **Universality of polygons:** Enables constructing triangles in any plane in space without exception.
- **Affine span:** Guarantees a two-dimensional coordinate framework over every plane $\pi$.
