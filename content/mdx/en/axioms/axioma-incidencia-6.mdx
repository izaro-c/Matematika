<Capitular letra="I" />f two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> of a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> belong to a <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink>, the sixth incidence axiom of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> asserts that every point of that line belongs to that plane. This postulate establishes the strict geometric flatness and rigidity of planar surfaces.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  For every <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> $l \in \mathcal{L}$ and every <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink> $\pi \in \mathcal{E}$, if there exist two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> $A, B \in l$ such that $A \in \pi$ and $B \in \pi$, then $l \subset \pi$:
  $$
  \forall l \in \mathcal{L}, \forall \pi \in \mathcal{E} \ \bigl((\exists A, B \in l \ (A \neq B \land A \in \pi \land B \in \pi)) \implies l \subset \pi\bigr)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Geometric significance and scope

Ensures strict spatial flatness: a plane cannot possess ripples, curvatures, or creases that would allow a line to cross two of its points without being wholly absorbed into it. It establishes a consistent structural link between 1D and 2D geometry.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Criterion for complete containment:** Checking membership of two distinct points suffices to establish containment of the entire line.
- **Maximal intersection of non-contained lines:** A line not wholly contained in a plane can intersect that plane in at most one single point.
- **Segment support:** Any line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segment</ConceptLink> $\overline{AB}$ whose endpoints lie in $\pi$ is completely contained in $\pi$.
