<Capitular letra="I" />f two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>planes</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia" isDependency={true}>are incident</ConceptLink> with the same <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>point</ConceptLink>, then they are incident with at least a second common point.

In the <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-incidencia">incidence system</ConceptLink>, this postulate describes the dimensional behavior of <ConceptLink targetId="espacio">space</ConceptLink>, preventing two distinct planes from intersecting at a single isolated point.

<Definicion title="Axiom I.7 (Intersection of Planes)">
Let <VisualBind element="plano-pi" color="azul">$\pi_1$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="plano-sigma" color="ocre">$\pi_2$</VisualBind> be two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="plano">planes</ConceptLink>. If both are <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incident</ConceptLink> with a <ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind>, there exists at least a second point <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> distinct from $A$ that is also incident with both planes:

$$
\forall \pi_1, \pi_2 \in \Pi, \, \forall A \in \mathcal{P} \ \Biggl(\Bigl(\pi_1 \neq \pi_2 \land A \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_1 \land A \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_2\Bigr) \implies \exists B \in \mathcal{P} \ \Bigl(B \neq A \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_1 \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \pi_2\Bigr)\Biggr)
$$
</Definicion>

<Nota>
This postulate strictly asserts the existence of a second common <ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink>. The deduction that the full intersection of the <ConceptLink targetId="traza">point traces</ConceptLink> of both <ConceptLink targetId="plano">planes</ConceptLink> is exactly a single <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> ($\operatorname{tr}(\pi_1) \cap \operatorname{tr}(\pi_2) = \operatorname{tr}(\overleftrightarrow{AB})$) constitutes the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-interseccion-planos">plane intersection line theorem</ConceptLink>, proved by combining this axiom with postulates <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-1">I.1</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-6">I.6</ConceptLink>, and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-5">I.5</ConceptLink>.
</Nota>
