<Capitular letra="O" />f three distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> situated on the same <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink>, at most one lies <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> the other two.

In the formulation of <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink> established by <ConceptLink targetId="david-hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink>, this postulate establishes the mutual exclusion of linear betweenness relations, preventing the <ConceptLink targetId="orden">order</ConceptLink> along a line from becoming cyclic or closing upon itself like a <ConceptLink targetId="circunferencia">circle</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Axiom II.3 (Exclusivity of Linear Betweenness)">
For any <ConceptLink targetId="terna">triple</ConceptLink> of mutually distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="pC" color="terracota">$C$</VisualBind> in $\mathcal{P}$ <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incident</ConceptLink> with the same <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="lineDE" color="carbon">$\ell$</VisualBind>, at most one of the betweenness relations $A * B * C$, $B * A * C$, or $A * C * B$ holds true:

$$
\forall A, B, C \in \mathcal{P} \ \Biggl( \Bigl(A \neq B \land B \neq C \land A \neq C \land \exists \ell \in \mathcal{L} \, (A \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land C \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell)\Bigr) \implies \neg\Bigl( (A * B * C \land B * A * C) \lor (A * B * C \land A * C * B) \lor (B * A * C \land A * C * B) \Bigr) \Biggr)
$$
</Definicion>

<Nota>
In conjunction with <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-1">Axiom II.1</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-2">Axiom II.2</ConceptLink>, it is deduced as the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-orden-tres-puntos">three-point linear order theorem</ConceptLink> that of three distinct <ConceptLink targetId="colinealidad">collinear</ConceptLink> points, exactly one lies between the other two. This property endows every line in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink> with a strict linear betweenness structure, from which a <ConceptLink targetId="orden-total">total order</ConceptLink> is induced upon selecting an <ConceptLink targetId="orientacion">orientation</ConceptLink> or <ConceptLink targetId="sentido">sense</ConceptLink>.
</Nota>
