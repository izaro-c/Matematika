<Capitular letra="O" />f any three distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> situated on a single <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink>, the third order axiom of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> establishes that at most one can lie <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> the other two. This condition prevents circular or cyclic orderings on geometric lines.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  For any three distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> $A, B, C$ belonging to a single <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> $l \in \mathcal{L}$, at most one betweenness relation holds simultaneously:
  $$
  \forall A, B, C \in l \ \bigl(A\text{-}B\text{-}C \implies (\neg B\text{-}A\text{-}C \land \neg A\text{-}C\text{-}B)\bigr)
  $$
  In conjunction with the other order axioms, exactly one of the three points lies between the other two.
</Definicion>

### Geometric significance and scope

This axiom guarantees the strict linearity and open topological nature of geometric lines. It prevents the ordering relation from closing in on itself (as occurs in projective cyclic orders on circles), establishing a strict total order in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Mutual exclusion:** Truth of $A\text{-}B\text{-}C$ automatically disproves $B\text{-}A\text{-}C$ and $A\text{-}C\text{-}B$.
- **Strict linear ordering:** Enables ordering any finite set of collinear points into a unique sequence $A_1\text{-}A_2\text{-}\dots\text{-}A_n$.
- **Non-cyclicity:** Forbids synthetic lines from closing topologically onto themselves without points at infinity.
