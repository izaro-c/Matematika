<Capitular letra="T" />he axiom of line completeness —framed in terms of the continuity principle of <ConceptLink targetId="dedekind">Richard Dedekind</ConceptLink> and formalized by <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink>— ensures the complete absence of "holes", gaps, or topological tears along the geometric line. It establishes that every continuous partition of <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> on a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> is generated by a unique boundary point.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  Let $l \in \mathcal{L}$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink>. If all points of $l$ are partitioned into two non-empty classes $\Sigma_1$ and $\Sigma_2$ ($l = \Sigma_1 \cup \Sigma_2$, $\Sigma_1 \cap \Sigma_2 = \emptyset$) such that no point of $\Sigma_1$ lies <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> two points of $\Sigma_2$ and vice versa, then there exists a unique <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>point</ConceptLink> $P \in l$ separating the two classes:
  $$
  \forall \text{ partition } (\Sigma_1, \Sigma_2) \text{ of } l, \ \exists! P \in l \ \bigl(\forall A \in \Sigma_1 \setminus \{P\}, \forall B \in \Sigma_2 \setminus \{P\} \implies A\text{-}P\text{-}B\bigr)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Geometric significance and scope

Guarantees the bijective and isomorphic correspondence between the synthetic geometric line and the real number continuum $\mathbb{R}$. It enables proving the existence of points of intersection between circles and secant lines, rigorously underpinning mathematical analysis within <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Perfect continuity:** Eliminates any infinitesimal gap or topological discontinuity along the geometric line.
- **Isomorphism with $\mathbb{R}$:** In combination with the <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-arquimedes">Archimedean axiom</ConceptLink>, ensures that the geometric line is homeomorphic and isomorphic to the Euclidean real line $\mathbb{R}$.
- **Curve intersections:** Guarantees that any line entering a circular region intersects its boundary circle.
