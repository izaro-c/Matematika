<Capitular letra="T" />here exist at least four non-<ConceptLink targetId="coplanaridad">coplanar</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink>; that is, points such that no <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink> is simultaneously <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia" isDependency={true}>incident</ConceptLink> with all four.

This axiom sets the lower bound on the <ConceptLink targetId="dimension">dimension</ConceptLink> of <ConceptLink targetId="espacio">space</ConceptLink>: it prevents all points from being incident with a single <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>. Together with the upper bound supplied by <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-7">Axiom I.7</ConceptLink>, incidence space turns out to be three-dimensional. It is the last of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">Hilbert</ConceptLink>'s axioms of <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incidence</ConceptLink>.
