<Capitular letra="E" />very <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> is <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia" isDependency={true}>incident</ConceptLink> with at least two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink>.

Together with <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-1">Axiom I.1</ConceptLink>, this axiom makes every line determined by its set of points: if two lines had the same <ConceptLink targetId="traza">point trace</ConceptLink>, they would share two distinct points and, by the uniqueness in I.1, they would coincide. The assignment $\ell \longmapsto \operatorname{tr}(\ell)$ is therefore <ConceptLink targetId="inyectividad">injective</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  For every <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="lineCD" color="carbon">$\ell$</VisualBind>, there exist at least two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="pA" color="ocre">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incident</ConceptLink> with $\ell$:
  $$
  \forall \ell \in \mathcal{L} \ \exists A, B \in \mathcal{P} \ (A \neq B \land A \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land B \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell)
  $$
  In terms of the <ConceptLink targetId="traza">point trace</ConceptLink> of the line, the axiom states that its <ConceptLink targetId="cardinalidad">cardinality</ConceptLink> satisfies $\lvert \operatorname{tr}(\ell) \rvert \ge 2$.
</Definicion>
