<Capitular letra="G" />iven any two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> in <ConceptLink targetId="espacio">space</ConceptLink>, the first incidence axiom formulated by <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> guarantees the existence and uniqueness of a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> passing through both. This postulate serves as the foundation for linear connection in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  Given any two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> $A, B \in \mathcal{S}$ with $A \neq B$, there exists a unique <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> $l \in \mathcal{L}$ incident with both $A$ and $B$:
  $$
  \forall A, B \in \mathcal{S} \ \bigl(A \neq B \implies \exists! l \in \mathcal{L} \ (A \in l \land B \in l)\bigr)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Visual representation and interaction

In the interactive simulation, points <VisualBind element="pA" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pB" color="terracota">$B$</VisualBind> uniquely determine the supporting line <VisualBind element="lineAB" color="carbon">$l$</VisualBind>. Dragging either point freely across the plane instantly recalculates the position and direction of the line, maintaining the strict dual membership condition.

<Separador />

### Geometric significance and scope

This axiom establishes that two points uniquely fix a direction and rectilinear trajectory. It eliminates the presence of redundant connecting curves or multiple straight lines joining the same two primitive points without the intervention of additional axioms.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Uniqueness of path:** Two distinct points cannot belong simultaneously to two distinct lines.
- **System independence:** Retains universal validity in both <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-euclidiano">Euclidean geometry</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-hiperbolico">hyperbolic geometry</ConceptLink>.
- **Foundation of connection:** Enables the definition of line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segment</ConceptLink> $\overline{AB}$ as a continuous subset of the supporting line.
