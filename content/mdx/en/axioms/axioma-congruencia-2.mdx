<Capitular letra="T" />he second congruence axiom of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> endows the congruence relation on line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> with the status of a formal equivalence relation. If two segments $\overline{CD}$ and $\overline{EF}$ are both congruent to a common segment $\overline{AB}$, they are necessarily <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruent</ConceptLink> to each other.

<Definicion title="Formal statement">
  For any three line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> $\overline{AB}, \overline{CD}, \overline{EF}$, if $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$ and $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}$, then $\overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF}$:
  $$
  (\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD} \land \overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}) \implies \overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF}
  $$
  Additionally, every segment is congruent to itself: $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{AB}$.
</Definicion>

### Geometric significance and scope

Guarantees that segment congruence partitions the set of all spatial segments into well-defined equivalence classes. Each equivalence class $[\overline{AB}]$ abstractly defines the synthetic concept of "length" in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Equivalence relation:** Satisfies reflexivity ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{AB}$), symmetry ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD} \implies \overline{CD} \cong \overline{AB}$), and transitivity.
- **Synthetic length classes:** Each equivalence class $[\overline{AB}]$ represents an invariant length measure under translations and isometries.
- **Metric consistency:** Prevents cumulative length drift across chained geometric deductions.
