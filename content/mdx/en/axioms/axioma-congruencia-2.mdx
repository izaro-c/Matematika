<Capitular letra="I" />f two <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>line segments</ConceptLink> are <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruent</ConceptLink> to the same third segment, then they are congruent to each other.

In the <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink> of <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink>, this postulate, together with the reflexivity established in <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-1">Axiom III.1</ConceptLink>, endows segment congruence with the formal structure of an <ConceptLink targetId="relacion-equivalencia">equivalence relation</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Axiom III.2 (Transitivity of Segment Congruence)">
For any <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="segAB" color="ocre">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="segCD" color="mora">$\overline{CD}$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="segEF" color="terracota">$\overline{EF}$</VisualBind> determined by <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> in the primitive <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domain</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$, if <VisualBind element="ABCD" color="mora">$\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="ABEF" color="terracota">$\overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}$</VisualBind>, then <VisualBind element="CDEF" color="pavo">$\overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF}$</VisualBind>:

$$
\forall A, B, C, D, E, F \in \mathcal{P} \ \Biggl( (A \neq B \land C \neq D \land E \neq F) \implies \Bigl( (\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD} \land \overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}) \implies \overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF} \Bigr) \Biggr)
$$
</Definicion>

<Nota>
From the reflexivity $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{AB}$ in <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-1">Axiom III.1</ConceptLink> and this postulate, <ConceptLink targetId="simetria">symmetry</ConceptLink> ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD} \implies \overline{CD} \cong \overline{AB}$) and canonical <ConceptLink targetId="transitividad">transitivity</ConceptLink> ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD} \land \overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF} \implies \overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}$) are formally deduced as <ConceptLink targetId="teorema">theorems</ConceptLink>, partitioning the set of segments into <ConceptLink targetId="clase-equivalencia">equivalence classes</ConceptLink> that ground the synthetic notion of <ConceptLink targetId="longitud">length</ConceptLink> in <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>.
</Nota>
