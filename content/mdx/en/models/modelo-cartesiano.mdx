<Capitular letra="T" />he Cartesian plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ constitutes the standard analytic model for two-dimensional <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-euclidiana">Euclidean geometry</ConceptLink>. By translates geometric objects into algebraic structures of ordered pairs over the field of <ConceptLink targetId="numero-real">real numbers</ConceptLink>, it grounds the consistency of synthetic geometry in mathematical analysis.

<Separador />

### Model definition

In the two-dimensional space $\mathbb{R}^2 = \{(x, y) \mid x, y \in \mathbb{R}\}$, synthetic geometric primitives are interpreted using set theory and linear algebra:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="punto">Points</ConceptLink>**: Individual elements <VisualBind element="A" color="terracota">$A(x_A, y_A)$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="B" color="musgo">$B(x_B, y_B)$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="P" color="ocre">$P(x_P, y_P)$</VisualBind> in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="recta">Lines</ConceptLink>**: Sets of points satisfying a linear equation of the form $ax + by + c = 0$, with $(a, b) \neq (0, 0)$. Line <VisualBind element="lineAB" color="musgo">$l$</VisualBind> contains points $A$ and $B$, while the <VisualBind element="parallelP" color="pavo">parallel line $l'$</VisualBind> passes through $P$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">Incidence</ConceptLink>**: A point $A$ lies on a line $l$ if and only if its coordinates satisfy the linear equation of $l$.
- **Euclidean <ConceptLink targetId="distancia">distance</ConceptLink>**: For any two points $A$ and $B$, the Euclidean metric is given by the Pythagorean distance formula:
  $$ d(A, B) = \sqrt{(x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2} $$

<Separador />

### Axiom verification

The Cartesian plane satisfies the complete <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-euclidiano">Euclidean axiomatic system</ConceptLink>:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">Incidence</ConceptLink> axioms**: For any two distinct points $A$ and $B$, there exists a unique linear equation (up to a non-zero scalar multiple) satisfied by both.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="orden">Order</ConceptLink> axioms**: The <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre">betweenness</ConceptLink> relation is metrically verified via the equality $d(A, B) + d(B, C) = d(A, C)$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides" isDependency={true}>Euclidean parallel axiom</ConceptLink>**: Given a line $l$ and an external point $P \notin l$, there exists a unique <ConceptLink targetId="paralelismo">parallel line</ConceptLink> $l'$ containing $P$ (lines sharing the identical algebraic slope $m = -a/b$).

<Separador />

### Properties

- **Completeness and continuity**: Inherits topological completeness directly from the continuous real field $\mathbb{R}$.
- **Isometric invariance**: Translations, rotations, and reflections preserve the metric $d(A, B)$ and the structure of <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink>.
