<Capitular letra="T" />he Cartesian plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ constitutes the canonical analytic model of two-dimensional Euclidean geometry. By rigorously mapping synthetic geometric entities into algebraic structures of ordered pairs over the field of real numbers $\mathbb{R}$, it grounds the consistency of synthetic geometry in mathematical analysis and arithmetic.

<Separador />

### Model Definition

In the two-dimensional space $\mathbb{R}^2 = \{(x, y) \mid x, y \in \mathbb{R}\}$, synthetic geometric primitives are interpreted through linear algebra and set theory:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="punto">Points</ConceptLink>**: Individual elements represented by their coordinates <VisualBind element="A" color="terracota">$A(x_A, y_A)$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="B" color="terracota">$B(x_B, y_B)$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="P" color="ocre">$P(x_P, y_P)$</VisualBind> in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="recta">Lines</ConceptLink>**: Subsets of points satisfying a linear equation of the form $ax + by + c = 0$, with $(a, b) \neq (0, 0)$. The <VisualBind element="lineAB" color="musgo">line $l$</VisualBind> passes through points $A$ and $B$, while the <VisualBind element="parallelP" color="canela">parallel line $l'$</VisualBind> passes through the exterior point $P$.
- **Incidence**: A point $A(x_A, y_A)$ is incident with a line $l: ax + by + c = 0$ if and only if $a x_A + b y_A + c = 0$.
- **Euclidean Distance**: For any two points $A$ and $B$, the Euclidean metric is given by the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-pitagoras">Pythagorean</ConceptLink> formula:
  $$ d(A, B) = \sqrt{(x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2} $$

### Verification of Axioms

The Cartesian plane satisfies the entirety of the <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-euclidiano">Euclidean axiomatic system</ConceptLink>:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-1">Incidence Axiom I-1</ConceptLink>**: Given any two distinct points $A(x_A, y_A)$ and $B(x_B, y_B)$, there exists a unique line defined by $\det \begin{pmatrix} x & y & 1 \\ x_A & y_A & 1 \\ x_B & y_B & 1 \end{pmatrix} = 0$.
- **Order Axioms**: The <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre">betweenness</ConceptLink> relation ($B \text{ lies between } A \text{ and } C$) is verified metrically via the identity $d(A, B) + d(B, C) = d(A, C)$ with $A \neq B \neq C$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides">Euclid's Parallel Axiom</ConceptLink>**: Given a line $l: ax + by + c = 0$ and an external point $P(x_P, y_P) \notin l$, there exists a unique <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel line</ConceptLink> $l': ax + by - (ax_P + by_P) = 0$ passing through $P$.

### Fundamental Properties

- **Metric Completeness**: Inherits the topological completeness and continuum order of the real field $\mathbb{R}$.
- **Isometric Invariance**: Rigid transformations (translations, rotations, and reflections) preserve distances, <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink>, and <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> relations between <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink>.
