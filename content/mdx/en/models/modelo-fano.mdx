<Capitular letra="T" />he Fano plane, denoted algebraically as $\mathrm{PG}(2, 2)$, constitutes the smallest <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-incidencia">finite incidence model</ConceptLink> and finite projective plane. Constructed over the finite field $\mathbb{F}_2 = \{0, 1\}$, it consists of exactly $7$ points and $7$ lines, exhibiting complete combinatorial symmetry where the Euclidean notion of parallelism vanishes entirely.

<Separador />

### Combinatorial Structure

The Fano configuration is formally defined using the 3-dimensional vector space $\mathbb{F}_2^3 \setminus \{(0, 0, 0)\}$:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="punto">Points</ConceptLink>**: The $7$ one-dimensional linear subspaces of $\mathbb{F}_2^3$: <VisualBind element="p1" color="terracota">point $1$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p2" color="terracota">point $2$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p3" color="terracota">point $3$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p4" color="terracota">point $4$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p5" color="terracota">point $5$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p6" color="terracota">point $6$</VisualBind>, and the <VisualBind element="p7" color="musgo">central point $7$</VisualBind>.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="recta">Lines</ConceptLink>**: The $7$ two-dimensional subspaces (planes), represented by triples of collinear points:
  - Three outer triangle edges: <VisualBind element="l1" color="carbon">$l_1 = \{1, 2, 4\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="l2" color="carbon">$l_2 = \{2, 3, 5\}$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="l3" color="carbon">$l_3 = \{3, 1, 6\}$</VisualBind>.
  - Three concurrent medians: <VisualBind element="l4" color="carbon">$l_4 = \{1, 5, 7\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="l5" color="carbon">$l_5 = \{2, 6, 7\}$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="l6" color="carbon">$l_6 = \{3, 4, 7\}$</VisualBind>.
  - The interior circle (projective non-Euclidean line): <VisualBind element="l7" color="musgo">$l_7 = \{4, 5, 6\}$</VisualBind>.

### Verification of Incidence Axioms

The Fano plane satisfies the core axioms of projective geometry and incidence:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-1">Incidence Axiom I-1</ConceptLink>**: Any two distinct points determine a unique line incident with both.
- **Projective Intersection Axiom**: Any two distinct lines intersect at exactly one point (there are no <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel lines</ConceptLink>).
- **Uniform Regularity**: Every line contains exactly $3$ points, and every point is incident with exactly $3$ lines.
- **Global Non-Collinearity**: There exist four points such that no three are collinear.

<Separador />

### Algebraic Properties

- **Projective Duality**: Swapping the designations of points and lines yields an incidence structure isomorphic to the original plane.
- **Automorphism Group**: Its symmetry group is the projective special linear group $\mathrm{PGL}(3, 2) \cong \mathrm{PSL}(2, 7)$ of order $168$, fundamental in finite group theory and error-correcting codes.
