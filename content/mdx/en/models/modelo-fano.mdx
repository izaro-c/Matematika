<Capitular letra="T" />he **Fano plane** (denoted $\mathrm{PG}(2, 2)$) is the smallest finite <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-incidencia">incidence model</ConceptLink> and projective plane that exists. It is composed of exactly 7 <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> and 7 <ConceptLink targetId="recta">lines</ConceptLink>, with a perfect combinatorial symmetry where any two lines always intersect at a single point.

<Definicion title="Combinatorial Structure">
  The Fano plane consists of:
  - **7 Points**: <VisualBind element="p1" color="terracota">1</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p2" color="terracota">2</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p3" color="terracota">3</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p4" color="terracota">4</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p5" color="terracota">5</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="p6" color="terracota">6</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="p7" color="musgo">7</VisualBind>.
  - **7 Lines** (each formed by a triple of points):
    - Three sides of the outer triangle: <VisualBind element="l1" color="carbon">$\{1, 2, 4\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="l2" color="carbon">$\{2, 3, 5\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="l3" color="carbon">$\{3, 1, 6\}$</VisualBind>.
    - Three concurrent median lines: <VisualBind element="l4" color="carbon">$\{1, 5, 7\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="l5" color="carbon">$\{2, 6, 7\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="l6" color="carbon">$\{3, 4, 7\}$</VisualBind>.
    - An inner circle (projective line): <VisualBind element="l7" color="musgo">$\{4, 5, 6\}$</VisualBind>.
</Definicion>

### Verification of axioms and properties

- **<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-1">Axiom I.1</ConceptLink>:** Any two points belong to a unique shared line.
- **Projective intersection:** Any two distinct lines intersect at exactly one point; there are no <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel lines</ConceptLink>.
- **Symmetric uniformity:** Every line contains exactly 3 points, and through every point pass exactly 3 lines.
