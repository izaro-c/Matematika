<Capitular letra="T" />wo <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto" isDependency={true}>sets</ConceptLink> $A$ and $B$ are **disjoint** (or mutually exclusive) if they share no elements in common, that is, if their <ConceptLink targetId="interseccion" isDependency={true}>intersection</ConceptLink> is the <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-vacio" isDependency={true}>empty set</ConceptLink>:
$$
A \cap B = \emptyset \iff \forall x \ (x \in A \implies x \notin B)
$$

<Definicion title="Family of Mutually Disjoint Sets (Pairwise Disjoint)">
  Let <VisualBind element="familia" color="carbon">$\{A_i\}_{i \in I}$</VisualBind> be a <ConceptLink targetId="familia-indexada" isDependency={true}>family of sets indexed</ConceptLink> by a set $I$. The family is said to be **mutually disjoint** (or **pairwise disjoint**) if the intersection <VisualBind element="interseccionesDosADos" color="mora">$A_i \cap A_j$</VisualBind> of any two members with distinct indices $i \neq j$ is empty:
  $$
  \forall i, j \in I \ (i \neq j \implies A_i \cap A_j = \emptyset)
  $$
</Definicion>

    <PropiedadItem 
      id="disjuncion-dos-a-dos-vs-global" 
      title="Pairwise Disjointness vs. Empty Global Intersection"
      formula="\lvert I \rvert \ge 2 \land \forall i, j \in I \, (i \neq j \implies A_i \cap A_j = \emptyset) \implies \bigcap_{i \in I} A_i = \emptyset"
    >
      If the index set has at least two elements ($\lvert I \rvert \ge 2$), being pairwise disjoint implies having empty global intersection, because $\bigcap_{k \in I} A_k \subseteq A_i \cap A_j$ for any $i \neq j$. With exactly two indices both conditions coincide. The converse fails from three on: <VisualBind element="conjuntoA1" color="terracota">$A_1 = \{1, 2\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="conjuntoA2" color="pavo">$A_2 = \{2, 3\}$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="conjuntoA3" color="ocre">$A_3 = \{1, 3\}$</VisualBind> have empty global intersection, yet they meet pairwise, since <VisualBind element="interseccionA1A2" color="mora">$A_1 \cap A_2 = \{2\}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="interseccionA1A3" color="mora">$A_1 \cap A_3 = \{1\}$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="interseccionA2A3" color="mora">$A_2 \cap A_3 = \{3\}$</VisualBind>.
    </PropiedadItem>
