<Capitular letra="T" />wo <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto">sets</ConceptLink> $A$ and $B$ are **disjoint** (or mutually exclusive) if they share no elements in common, that is, if their intersection is the empty set:
$$
A \cap B = \emptyset
$$
