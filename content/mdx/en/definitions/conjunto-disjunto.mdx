<Capitular letra="T" />wo <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto" isDependency={true}>sets</ConceptLink> $A$ and $B$ are **disjoint** (or mutually exclusive) if they share no elements in common, that is, if their <ConceptLink targetId="interseccion" isDependency={true}>intersection</ConceptLink> is the <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-vacio">empty set</ConceptLink>:
$$
A \cap B = \emptyset \iff \forall x \ (x \in A \implies x \notin B)
$$

<Definicion title="Family of Mutually Disjoint Sets (Pairwise Disjoint)">
  Let $\{A_i\}_{i \in I}$ be a family of sets indexed by a set $I$. The family is said to be **mutually disjoint** (or **pairwise disjoint**) if any two distinct sets in the family have empty intersection:
  $$
  \forall i, j \in I \ (i \neq j \implies A_i \cap A_j = \emptyset)
  $$
</Definicion>
