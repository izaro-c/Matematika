<Capitular letra="G" />eometric congruence is the fundamental <ConceptLink targetId="relacion-equivalencia">equivalence relation</ConceptLink> between figures in the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink> or space that share identical shape and size. Two figures are congruent if there exists a rigid <ConceptLink targetId="transformacion-geometrica">isometry</ConceptLink> (translation, rotation, or reflection) that maps one onto the other while preserving all inter-<ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink> distances and <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angle</ConceptLink> measures.

<Definicion title="">
  Let $F_1, F_2 \subset \mathcal{E}$ be two point subsets of a Euclidean metric space $\mathcal{E}$. Figures $F_1$ and $F_2$ are defined to be congruent, denoted <VisualBind element="figura-1" color="musgo">$F_1$</VisualBind> $\cong$ <VisualBind element="figura-2" color="pavo">$F_2$</VisualBind>, if and only if there exists a bijective transformation $f: \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}$ such that $f(F_1) = F_2$ preserving all metric distances between points $A, B \in \mathcal{E}$:
  $$
  F_1 \cong F_2 \iff \exists f: \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E} \text{ such that } f(F_1) = F_2 \land \forall A, B \in \mathcal{E}, \|f(B) - f(A)\| = \|B - A\|
  $$
  For line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$, this implies equality of their scalar lengths $d(A, B) = d(C, D)$.
</Definicion>

### Triangle congruence criteria

In <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-euclidiana">planar geometry</ConceptLink>, the congruence of <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink> is completely determined by minimal sets of conditions regarding their <ConceptLink targetId="lado">sides</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink>:

| Criterion | Axiom / theorem name | Minimal geometric condition |
|---|---|---|
| SAS | <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-5">Side-Angle-Side</ConceptLink> | Two sides and the included angle are congruent |
| ASA | <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-congruencia-ala">Angle-Side-Angle</ConceptLink> | One side and the two adjacent angles are congruent |
| SSS | Side-Side-Side Criterion | All three pairs of corresponding sides are congruent |

### Properties

- **<ConceptLink targetId="relacion-equivalencia">Equivalence relation</ConceptLink>**: Congruence is reflexive ($F_1 \cong F_1$), symmetric ($F_1 \cong F_2 \implies F_2 \cong F_1$), and transitive ($F_1 \cong F_2 \land F_2 \cong F_3 \implies F_1 \cong F_3$).
- **Invariance of measure**: If two measurable planar figures are congruent $F_1 \cong F_2$, their area measures satisfy strict equality $\mu(F_1) = \mu(F_2)$.
- **Preservation of collinearity**: Every isometry preserves the <ConceptLink targetId="colinealidad">collinearity</ConceptLink> of points and the structure of <ConceptLink targetId="recta">lines</ConceptLink>.
