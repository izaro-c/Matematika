<Capitular letra="G" />eometric congruence is the primitive relation of rigidity and equivalence in synthetic geometry that formalizes the notion of equal size and shape between figures. In Hilbert's axiomatic system, congruence is postulated as a primitive relation for pairs of <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$) and pairs of <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> ($\angle ABC \cong \angle DEF$).

<Definicion title="">
  Congruence ($\cong$) is a primitive equivalence relation on pairs of geometric configurations in a space $\mathcal{E}$. In analytic Euclidean geometry, two subsets $F_1, F_2 \subset \mathcal{E}$ are congruent if and only if there exists a bijective isometry $f: \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}$ such that $f(F_1) = F_2$ and for all pairs of points $A, B \in \mathcal{E}$:
  $$
  d(f(A), f(B)) = d(A, B)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Hilbert's congruence axioms (Group III)

The behavior of the congruence relation is governed by the six axioms of Group III:
- **Segment transport**: Given a segment $\overline{AB}$ and a ray originating at $A'$, there exists a unique point $B'$ on the ray such that $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{A'B'}$.
- **Transitivity of segments**: If $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$ and $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}$, then $\overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF}$.
- **Addition of segments**: If $A * B * C$, $A' * B' * C'$, $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{A'B'}$, and $\overline{BC} \cong \overline{B'C'}$, then $\overline{AC} \cong \overline{A'C'}$.
- **Angle transport**: Given an angle $\angle ABC$ and a reference ray on a given half-plane, there exists a unique congruent angle.
- **SAS criterion**: If two triangles have two sides and the included angle respectively congruent, then their remaining corresponding angles are congruent.

### Triangle congruence criteria

In the Euclidean plane, congruence between two <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink> is established through the standard criteria:
- **SAS** (Side-Angle-Side): Two sides and the included angle are congruent.
- **ASA** (Angle-Side-Angle): One side and both adjacent angles are congruent.
- **SSS** (Side-Side-Side): All three corresponding side pairs are congruent.
