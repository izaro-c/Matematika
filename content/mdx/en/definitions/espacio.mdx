<Capitular letra="S" />**pace** is the total universe of discourse and geometric realm in which all relations of a mathematical theory are interpreted. It represents the highest tier in geometry's elementary dimensional hierarchy: the <ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink> (<ConceptLink targetId="dimension">dimension</ConceptLink> 0), the <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> (<ConceptLink targetId="dimension">dimension</ConceptLink> 1), the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink> (<ConceptLink targetId="dimension">dimension</ConceptLink> 2), and **space** (<ConceptLink targetId="dimension">dimension</ConceptLink> 3 or higher).

In an <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-axiomatico">axiomatic system</ConceptLink>, space possesses no pre-established substantial properties; its behavior and geometry are governed entirely by the postulated <ConceptLink targetId="axioma">axioms</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Geometric Definition and Universe of Discourse">
  In <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink>, **three-dimensional space** is the universe of discourse $\mathcal{U} = \mathcal{P} \sqcup \mathcal{L} \sqcup \Pi$ formed by the disjoint union of the <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domains</ConceptLink> of <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$, <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>lines</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{L}$, and <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>planes</ConceptLink> $\Pi$, formally characterized by two fundamental <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incidence</ConceptLink> conditions:
  1. **Three-dimensionality:** There exist at least four non-<ConceptLink targetId="coplanaridad">coplanar</ConceptLink> points (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-8" isDependency={true}>Axiom I.8</ConceptLink>).
  2. **Planar intersection:** If two distinct planes share a point, they share at least a second common point (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-7" isDependency={true}>Axiom I.7</ConceptLink>), which entails that their complete mutual intersection is precisely a line (theorem derived from I.7, I.1, I.6, and I.4-I.5).
</Definicion>

### Varieties of geometric spaces and their signatures

- <ConceptLink targetId="espacio-incidencia">**Incidence space**</ConceptLink>: $\mathcal{S}_{\text{inc}} = (\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \Pi; \, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\mathcal{L}}, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\Pi})$, minimal structure governed solely by positional membership relations (<ConceptLink targetId="sistema-incidencia">incidence system</ConceptLink>).
- <ConceptLink targetId="espacio-absoluto">**Absolute space**</ConceptLink>: $\mathcal{S}_{\text{abs}} = (\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \Pi; \, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\mathcal{L}}, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\Pi}, \mathbf{B}, \equiv_{\text{seg}}, \equiv_{\angle})$, incorporating <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre">order</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink>, and <ConceptLink targetId="continuidad">continuity</ConceptLink> (<ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>).
- <ConceptLink targetId="espacio-euclidiano">**Euclidean space**</ConceptLink>: $\mathcal{S}_{\text{euc}} = (\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \Pi; \, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\mathcal{L}}, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\Pi}, \mathbf{B}, \equiv_{\text{seg}}, \equiv_{\angle})$ such that $\mathcal{S}_{\text{euc}} \models \mathcal{T}_{\text{abs}} \cup \{\text{Axiom IV}\}$, flat zero-curvature space governed by <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides">Euclid's parallel postulate</ConceptLink>.
- <ConceptLink targetId="espacio-hiperbolico">**Hyperbolic space**</ConceptLink>: $\mathcal{S}_{\text{hip}} = (\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \Pi; \, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\mathcal{L}}, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\Pi}, \mathbf{B}, \equiv_{\text{seg}}, \equiv_{\angle})$ such that $\mathcal{S}_{\text{hip}} \models \mathcal{T}_{\text{abs}} \cup \{\text{Axiom IV}_{\text{hip}}\}$, homogeneous space of constant negative curvature governed by the <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-hiperbolico">hyperbolic postulate</ConceptLink>.
- <ConceptLink targetId="espacio-proyectivo">**Projective space**</ConceptLink>: $\mathcal{S}_{\text{proy}} = (\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \Pi; \, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\mathcal{L}}, \mathbf{I}_{\mathcal{P}\Pi})$, geometric extension where <ConceptLink targetId="coplanaridad">coplanar</ConceptLink> lines always intersect at a common point.
