<Capitular letra="T" />he order of <ConceptLink targetId="angulo" isDependency={true}>angles</ConceptLink> is the relation that compares the opening of two angles synthetically, without resorting to numerical measures or trigonometric functions.

This relation is constructed by combining the angle transport postulated in <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-4" isDependency={true}>Axiom III.4</ConceptLink> with the notion of the <ConceptLink targetId="interior-angulo">angle interior</ConceptLink>. By placing one angle on an <ConceptLink targetId="lado">arm</ConceptLink> of the other within the same <ConceptLink targetId="semiplano">half-plane</ConceptLink>, the containment of its remaining arm in the interior defines a synthetic <ConceptLink targetId="orden">strict order</ConceptLink> compatible with <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of the order of angles">
Let $\angle AOB$ and $\angle A'O'B'$ be two <ConceptLink targetId="angulo" isDependency={true}>angles</ConceptLink> determined by pairs of non-collinear <ConceptLink targetId="semirrecta" isDependency={true}>rays</ConceptLink> with origins $O$ and $O'$ respectively.

By <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-4" isDependency={true}>Axiom III.4</ConceptLink>, there exists a unique ray $\overrightarrow{O'B''}$ with origin $O'$, lying in the <ConceptLink targetId="semiplano">half-plane</ConceptLink> of the line $\overleftrightarrow{O'A'}$ containing $B'$, such that:
$$
\angle AOB \cong \angle A'O'B''
$$

We say that the angle $\angle AOB$ is **strictly less than** the angle $\angle A'O'B'$, denoted $\angle AOB < \angle A'O'B'$ (or $\angle\alpha < \angle\beta$), if the ray $\overrightarrow{O'B''}$, excluding its origin, is contained in the <ConceptLink targetId="interior-angulo">interior</ConceptLink> of $\angle A'O'B'$:
$$
\angle AOB < \angle A'O'B' \iff (\overrightarrow{O'B''} \setminus \{O'\}) \subseteq \operatorname{Int}(\angle A'O'B')
$$

Conversely, we say that $\angle A'O'B'$ is **strictly greater than** $\angle AOB$, denoted $\angle A'O'B' > \angle AOB$, if and only if $\angle AOB < \angle A'O'B'$.
</Definicion>
