<Capitular letra="A" /> line segment is a bounded, connected subset of a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> bounded by two distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> called endpoints. Unlike an infinite line, it has a well-defined metric <ConceptLink targetId="distancia">length</ConceptLink> and serves as the fundamental building block for constructing <ConceptLink targetId="poligono">polygons</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="">
  Given two points <VisualBind element="A" color="musgo">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="B" color="musgo">$B$</VisualBind> on a supporting <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="carrier" color="carbon">$\ell$</VisualBind>, the closed line segment <VisualBind element="segment" color="musgo">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind> is the set consisting of $A$, $B$, and all points $P$ lying <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre">between</ConceptLink> $A$ and $B$:
  $$
  \overline{AB} = \{ P \in \ell \mid P = (1 - t)A + tB, \, t \in [0, 1] \}
  $$
  The scalar distance $d(A, B) = \|B - A\|$ defines the <VisualBind element="length" color="mora">length of the segment</VisualBind>.
</Definicion>

<Separador />

### Segment typology according to bounds and positioning

Line segments are formally classified based on endpoint inclusion or their relative geometric placement:

| Classification | Set definition | Geometric feature |
|---|---|---|
| Closed segment $\overline{AB}$ | $\{ P \in \ell \mid t \in [0, 1] \}$ | Includes both endpoints $A$ and $B$ |
| Open segment $(AB)$ | $\{ P \in \ell \mid t \in (0, 1) \}$ | Excludes both endpoints $A$ and $B$ |
| Consecutive segments | $\overline{AB} \cap \overline{BC} = \{B\}$ | Share exactly one endpoint $B$ |

<Separador />

### Properties

- **<ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">Segment congruence</ConceptLink>**: Two line segments are congruent ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$) if and only if they have equal metric length.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="region-convexa">Convexity</ConceptLink>**: Every line segment is a strictly connected and convex set in $\mathbb{R}^n$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="punto-medio">Midpoint existence</ConceptLink>**: There exists a unique point $M \in \overline{AB}$ such that $\overline{AM} \cong \overline{MB}$.
