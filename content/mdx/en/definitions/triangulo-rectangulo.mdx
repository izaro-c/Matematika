<Capitular letra="A" /> **right triangle** is a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo" isDependency={true}>triangle</ConceptLink> characterized by having one of its <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">interior angles</ConceptLink> congruent to a <ConceptLink targetId="angulo-recto" isDependency={true}>right angle</ConceptLink>, bounded by two meeting <ConceptLink targetId="cateto">legs</ConceptLink> and an opposite <ConceptLink targetId="hipotenusa">hypotenuse</ConceptLink>.

This figure is established within <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink> without relying on metric angle measures, providing the structural basis for specialized right triangle congruence criteria and the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-pitagoras">Pythagorean theorem</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal Definition of Right Triangle">
Let $A, B, C \in \mathcal{P}$ be three non-<ConceptLink targetId="colinealidad">collinear</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> in a <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink> $\pi \in \Pi$, determining the <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo" isDependency={true}>triangle</ConceptLink> $\triangle ABC = \overline{AB} \cup \overline{BC} \cup \overline{CA}$.

The triangle $\triangle ABC$ is said to be a **right triangle** with right <ConceptLink targetId="vertice">vertex</ConceptLink> at $C$ if and only if the <ConceptLink targetId="secante">secant</ConceptLink> supporting <ConceptLink targetId="recta">lines</ConceptLink> $\overleftrightarrow{CA}$ and $\overleftrightarrow{CB}$ are <ConceptLink targetId="perpendicular" isDependency={true}>perpendicular</ConceptLink>:

$$
\triangle ABC \text{ is a right triangle at } C \iff \overleftrightarrow{CA} \perp \overleftrightarrow{CB} \iff \operatorname{Recto}(\angle BCA)
$$

The side opposite the right vertex, $\overline{AB}$, is called the <ConceptLink targetId="hipotenusa">hypotenuse</ConceptLink>, and the two sides meeting at $C$, $\overline{CA}$ and $\overline{CB}$, are called <ConceptLink targetId="cateto">legs</ConceptLink>.
</Definicion>

    </PropiedadItem>
