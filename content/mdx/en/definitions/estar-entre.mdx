<Capitular letra="T" />he betweenness relation, denoted formally as lying between, is a fundamental primitive ternary relation in David Hilbert's synthetic axiomatic geometry. It equips <ConceptLink targetId="recta">lines</ConceptLink> with a strict, dense linear order without presupposing real numbers or external metric concepts.

<Definicion title="">
  Let <VisualBind element="pA" color="musgo">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="ocre">$B$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="pC" color="musgo">$C$</VisualBind> be three distinct <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> lying on the same <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="lineAC" color="carbon">$\ell$</VisualBind>. The ternary relation $A * B * C$ ($B$ is between $A$ and $C$) is a primitive relation governed by Hilbert's order axioms. In a Euclidean metric setting, it is equivalent to the additivity of the lengths of <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="segAB" color="pavo">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="segBC" color="terracota">$\overline{BC}$</VisualBind> relative to <VisualBind element="segAC" color="musgo">$\overline{AC}$</VisualBind>:
  $$
  A * B * C \iff d(A, B) + d(B, C) = d(A, C)
  $$
</Definicion>

### Hilbert's order axioms (Group II)

Linear ordering along a line is formally grounded on the following betweenness axioms:
- **Symmetry**: If $A * B * C$, then $C * B * A$.
- **Extension**: Given points $A$ and $C$, there exists at least one point $D$ such that $A * C * D$.
- **Exclusivity**: Of any three distinct collinear points, at most one lies between the other two.
- **Pasch's Axiom**: A line intersecting the interior of one side of a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink> without passing through its vertices necessarily intersects the interior of another side.

### Analytic geometry

On the real line $\mathbb{R}$, given three distinct real numbers $x_A, x_B, x_C$, the relation is expressed algebraically:
$$
A * B * C \iff (x_A < x_B < x_C) \lor (x_C < x_B < x_A)
$$
In $\mathbb{R}^n$, this is equivalent to $B = (1 - t)A + tC$ for some $t \in (0, 1)$.
