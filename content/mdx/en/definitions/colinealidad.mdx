<Capitular letra="C" />**ollinearity** is the relation of <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia" isDependency={true}>incidence</ConceptLink> satisfied by a set of <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> when there exists at least one <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> with which all of them are incident.

By <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-1" isDependency={true}>Axiom I.1</ConceptLink>, any two distinct points determine a unique line and are therefore always collinear with each other. The notion acquires deductive relevance when considering three or more points: the existence of non-collinear triples postulates that the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink> does not degenerate into a single line (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-3">Axiom I.3</ConceptLink>), enables the determination of planes in <ConceptLink targetId="espacio">space</ConceptLink> (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-incidencia-4">Axiom I.4</ConceptLink>), and constitutes the indispensable condition for defining the <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink> and the <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angle</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of collinearity and non-collinearity">
Let $(\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{L}, \mathbf{I})$ be an <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-incidencia">incidence system</ConceptLink> where $\mathcal{P} \cap \mathcal{L} = \emptyset$. A non-empty set of points $\{P_1, P_2, \dots, P_n\} \subset \mathcal{P}$ is **collinear** if there exists a line $\ell \in \mathcal{L}$ such that each point is incident with it:

$$
\operatorname{col}(P_1, P_2, \dots, P_n) \iff \exists \ell \in \mathcal{L} \; \forall i \in \{1, \dots, n\} \, (P_i \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell)
$$

In particular, three points <VisualBind element="pA" color="musgo">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="pB" color="musgo">$B$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="pC" color="musgo">$C$</VisualBind> are collinear with respect to the line <VisualBind element="lineL" color="carbon">$\ell$</VisualBind> if and only if all three are incident with it:

$$
\operatorname{col}(A, B, C) \iff \exists \ell \in \mathcal{L} \, (A \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell \land B \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell \land C \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell)
$$

Conversely, the points $A$, $B$, and <VisualBind element="pD" color="terracota">$D$</VisualBind> are **non-collinear** if there exists no line in $\mathcal{L}$ that is simultaneously incident with all three:

$$
\neg\operatorname{col}(A, B, D) \iff \forall \ell \in \mathcal{L} \, \neg(A \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell \land B \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell \land D \,\mathbf{I}\, \ell)
$$
</Definicion>
