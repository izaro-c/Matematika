<Capitular letra="G" />eometric similarity is an <ConceptLink targetId="relacion-equivalencia">equivalence relation</ConceptLink> between figures in the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink> or space that preserves identical shape while permitting variations in global scale. Two figures are similar when there exists a similarity transformation (the composition of an <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">isometry</ConceptLink> and a homothety) that maintains proportional distances between all their <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> and equates corresponding <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="">
  Let $F_1, F_2 \subset \mathcal{E}$ be two subsets of points in a Euclidean metric space $\mathcal{E}$. Figures $F_1$ and $F_2$ are defined to be similar, denoted $F_1 \sim F_2$, if and only if there exists a positive scale factor $k > 0$ and a bijective transformation $g: \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}$ such that $g(F_1) = F_2$ satisfying, for every pair of points $A, B \in \mathcal{E}$:
  $$
  F_1 \sim F_2 \iff \exists k > 0, \exists g: \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E} \text{ s.t. } g(F_1) = F_2 \land \forall A, B \in \mathcal{E}, \|g(B) - g(A)\| = k \cdot \|B - A\|
  $$
  The scalar $k$ represents the scale factor or constant <ConceptLink targetId="koziente">ratio</ConceptLink> of similarity between the lengths of corresponding line <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> (<ConceptLink targetId="teorema-tales">Thales' theorem</ConceptLink>).
</Definicion>

### Triangle similarity criteria

In <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-euclidiana">planar geometry</ConceptLink>, the similarity of two <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink> is established by any of the following equivalent minimal criteria:

| Criterion | Formulation | Proportionality condition |
|---|---|---|
| AA | Angle-Angle | Two pairs of corresponding angles are <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruent</ConceptLink> |
| SAS | Side-Angle-Side | One congruent angle with equal <ConceptLink targetId="koziente">ratio</ConceptLink> for adjacent sides |
| SSS | Side-Side-Side | The <ConceptLink targetId="koziente">ratio</ConceptLink> of all three pairs of corresponding sides equals $k$ |

### Properties

- **<ConceptLink targetId="relacion-equivalencia">Equivalence relation</ConceptLink>**: Similarity is reflexive ($F_1 \sim F_1$), symmetric ($F_1 \sim F_2 \implies F_2 \sim F_1$), and transitive ($F_1 \sim F_2 \land F_2 \sim F_3 \implies F_1 \sim F_3$).
- **Relation to congruence**: Any pair of congruent figures $F_1 \cong F_2$ is a special case of similarity with scale factor $k = 1$.
- **Area scaling**: If two planar figures are similar with scale factor $k$, their area measures satisfy the quadratic relation $\mu(F_2) = k^2 \cdot \mu(F_1)$.
