<Capitular letra="A" /> line is a continuous one-dimensional geometric entity that extends indefinitely in both directions without curvature. In synthetic geometry, it constitutes an undefined primitive notion that, together with the <ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink> and the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>, underpins the axiomatic structure of incidence and order.

<Definicion title="">
  Given two distinct points <VisualBind element="pA" color="musgo">$A$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pB" color="musgo">$B$</VisualBind> in an affine or Euclidean space $\mathcal{E}$, the line <VisualBind element="lineAB" color="musgo">$\ell = \overleftrightarrow{AB}$</VisualBind> is the one-dimensional continuous set formed by all affine combinations of $A$ and $B$:
  $$
  \ell = \{ P \in \mathcal{E} \mid P = (1 - t)A + tB, \, t \in \mathbb{R} \}
  $$
</Definicion>

### Synthetic geometry

In Hilbert's formal framework, lines are elements of the second fundamental set governed by the axioms of incidence and order:
- **Uniqueness of incidence**: Two distinct points determine a unique line.
- **Linear continuity**: Any point on a line partitions the line into two disjoint <ConceptLink targetId="semirrecta">rays</ConceptLink>.
- **Betweenness**: The <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre">betweenness</ConceptLink> relation establishes a strict, dense linear order among the points of the line.

### Analytic geometry

In the Cartesian plane $\mathbb{R}^2$, a line is represented by its general implicit equation or parametric vector form:
- **General equation**: $Ax + By + C = 0$, with $(A, B) \neq (0, 0)$.
- **Vector form**: $\vec{r}(t) = \vec{p}_0 + t\vec{v}$, where $\vec{p}_0$ is an anchor point and $\vec{v} \neq \vec{0}$ is the direction vector.
- **Slope-intercept form**: $y = mx + b$, where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept.

### Topology

Topologically, any Euclidean line equipped with the standard topology is homeomorphic to the real line $\mathbb{R}$. It constitutes a complete, connected, non-compact metric space of topological dimension $1$.
