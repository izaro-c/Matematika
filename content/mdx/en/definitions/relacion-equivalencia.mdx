<Capitular letra="A" />n **equivalence relation** is a homogeneous binary relation that formalizes the abstract concept of indistinguishability or equality under a given criterion, simultaneously combining reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity.

It enables the abstraction of particular features of objects to group them into homogeneous classes, transforming strict equality into a modular notion of mathematical classification.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of equivalence relation">
  Let $A$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto" isDependency={true}>set</ConceptLink>. A <ConceptLink targetId="relacion-binaria">binary relation</ConceptLink> $\sim \ \subseteq A \times A$ is an **equivalence relation** on $A$ if and only if it satisfies the three axiomatic properties:
  1. **<ConceptLink targetId="relacion-reflexiva" isDependency={true}>Reflexivity</ConceptLink>:** Every element is related to itself:
     $$
     \forall x \in A, \quad x \sim x
     $$
  2. **<ConceptLink targetId="relacion-simetrica" isDependency={true}>Symmetry</ConceptLink>:** The relationship is bidirectional and reciprocal:
     $$
     \forall x, y \in A, \quad x \sim y \implies y \sim x
     $$
  3. **<ConceptLink targetId="relacion-transitiva" isDependency={true}>Transitivity</ConceptLink>:** The relationship is transmitted through intermediate elements:
     $$
     \forall x, y, z \in A, \quad (x \sim y \land y \sim z) \implies x \sim z
     $$
  
  Given an element $x \in A$, its **equivalence class** is the subset $[x]_\sim = \{y \in A \mid x \sim y\}$. The set of all equivalence classes forms the **quotient set** $A / \sim \ = \{[x]_\sim \mid x \in A\}$, which generates a perfect <ConceptLink targetId="particion">partition</ConceptLink> of $A$ into non-empty, pairwise <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-disjunto">disjoint</ConceptLink> subsets covering $A$.
</Definicion>

### Core applications in geometry

In the construction of geometric theories, equivalence relations organize the structure of space:

- **Structure of congruence:** Geometric <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> $\cong$ on segments (grounded in axioms <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-1">III.1</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-2">III.2</ConceptLink>) and on angles is an equivalence relation; each equivalence class represents the intrinsic length of the segment or the opening of the angle.
- **Sheaves of parallels:** In the Euclidean plane, <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallelism</ConceptLink> $\parallel$ is an equivalence relation on the set of lines; each equivalence class $[\ell]_\parallel$ defines a direction in the plane (a parallel pencil of lines).
