<Capitular letra="A" /> **binary operation** (or internal law of composition) on a non-empty <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto" isDependency={true}>set</ConceptLink> is a <ConceptLink targetId="funcion" isDependency={true}>function</ConceptLink> that assigns to each <ConceptLink targetId="par-ordenado">ordered pair</ConceptLink> of elements of the set a unique result belonging to that set. It constitutes the primary building block of <ConceptLink targetId="algebra-abstracta">abstract algebra</ConceptLink>, allowing the articulation of structures such as <ConceptLink targetId="semigrupo">semigroups</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="monoide">monoids</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="grupo">groups</ConceptLink>, and <ConceptLink targetId="cuerpo">fields</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of binary operation">
  Let $S$ be a non-empty <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto" isDependency={true}>set</ConceptLink>. An **internal binary operation** on $S$ is a <ConceptLink targetId="funcion" isDependency={true}>function</ConceptLink> from the <ConceptLink targetId="producto-cartesiano" isDependency={true}>Cartesian product</ConceptLink> $S \times S$ to $S$
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  *\colon S \times S &\longrightarrow S \\
  (a, b) &\longmapsto a * b
  \end{aligned}
  $$
  that associates with each ordered pair $(a, b) \in S \times S$ a unique element $a * b \in S$.
</Definicion>

The membership of the result in the set $S$ itself expresses the closure property:
$$
\forall a, b \in S, \quad a * b \in S
$$
Under this functional formulation, closure is intrinsically ensured by specifying $S$ as the <ConceptLink targetId="codominio">codomain</ConceptLink> of the mapping.

<Separador />

### Properties of a binary operation

A binary operation $*$ on $S$ can exhibit algebraic properties that govern the global structure of the pair $(S, *)$:

- **<ConceptLink targetId="asociatividad">Associativity</ConceptLink>:** Grouping does not alter the result: $\forall a, b, c \in S, \ (a * b) * c = a * (b * c)$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="conmutatividad">Commutativity</ConceptLink>:** The order of the operands does not alter the result: $\forall a, b \in S, \ a * b = b * a$.
- **Existence of <ConceptLink targetId="elemento-neutro">identity element</ConceptLink>:** There exists an element $e \in S$ such that $\forall a \in S, \ a * e = e * a = a$.
- **Existence of <ConceptLink targetId="elemento-inverso">inverse element</ConceptLink>:** Given an identity $e$, each $a \in S$ admits an element $a^{-1} \in S$ such that $a * a^{-1} = a^{-1} * a = e$.
