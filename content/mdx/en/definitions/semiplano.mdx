<Capitular letra="A" /> **half-plane** is either of the two regions into which a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> divides the points of a <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink>. The dividing line is called the **boundary** or edge and is not part of the half-plane.

In <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink>, the partition of the plane into exactly two <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-disjunto">disjoint</ConceptLink> regions is a direct consequence of <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-4" isDependency={true}>Pasch's Axiom</ConceptLink> (Axiom II.4 of order).

<Definicion title="Formal Definition">
  Let $\pi$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="plano" isDependency={true}>plane</ConceptLink> and let $\ell$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> contained in $\pi$. The line $\ell$ divides the <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> of $\pi \setminus \ell$ into two <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-disjunto">disjoint sets</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{H}_1$ and $\mathcal{H}_2$, called **half-planes**, determined by:
  $$
  A, B \in \mathcal{H}_1 \iff \overline{AB} \cap \ell = \emptyset
  $$
  $$
  A \in \mathcal{H}_1, \, C \in \mathcal{H}_2 \iff \overline{AC} \cap \ell \neq \emptyset
  $$
  where $\overline{AB}$ denotes the <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>segment</ConceptLink> joining points $A$ and $B$.
</Definicion>

  <PropiedadesGrupo title="Fundamental Geometric Properties">
    <PropiedadItem id="conjunto-convexo" title="Convexity">
      Every half-plane is a <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-convexo">convex set</ConceptLink>: if it contains any two points, it entirely contains the segment joining them.
    </PropiedadItem>
    <PropiedadItem id="angulo" title="Interior polygonal regions">
      The intersection of two or more half-planes whose boundaries cross allows the formal definition of the interior region of an <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angle</ConceptLink>, a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink>, and any <ConceptLink targetId="poligono">polygon</ConceptLink>.
    </PropiedadItem>
  </PropiedadesGrupo>
