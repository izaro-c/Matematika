<Capitular letra="A" /> group is the foundational algebraic structure that mathematically formalizes the concepts of symmetry, transformation, and invariance. It consists of a set equipped with an internal <ConceptLink targetId="operacion-binaria">binary operation</ConceptLink> that satisfies four fundamental axioms: closure, associativity, existence of an identity element, and existence of inverse elements.

<Definicion title="">
  A group $(G, \cdot)$ is an ordered pair consisting of a non-empty set $G$ and an internal <ConceptLink targetId="operacion-binaria" isDependency={true}>binary operation</ConceptLink> $\cdot: G \times G \to G$ satisfying the following axioms for all elements $a, b, c \in G$:
  1. **Closure**: $\forall a, b \in G, \; a \cdot b \in G$.
  2. **Associativity**: $\forall a, b, c \in G, \; (a \cdot b) \cdot c = a \cdot (b \cdot c)$.
  3. **Identity element**: $\exists e \in G \text{ such that } \forall a \in G, \; a \cdot e = e \cdot a = a$.
  4. **Inverse element**: $\forall a \in G, \; \exists a^{-1} \in G \text{ such that } a \cdot a^{-1} = a^{-1} \cdot a = e$.
</Definicion>

### Structural classification of groups

Groups are classified according to the commutativity of their operation and the cardinality of their underlying set:

| Group type | Algebraic condition | Representative example |
|---|---|---|
| Abelian (Commutative) | $\forall a, b \in G, \; a \cdot b = b \cdot a$ | Integers $(\mathbb{Z}, +)$ under addition |
| Non-abelian | $\exists a, b \in G \text{ such that } a \cdot b \neq b \cdot a$ | Invertible matrices $(\text{GL}_n(\mathbb{R}), \cdot)$ with $n \ge 2$ |
| Finite | Cardinality $\lvert G \rvert$ is finite | Symmetric group $S_n$ on $n$ elements ($\lvert S_n \rvert = n!$) |
| Infinite | Cardinality $\lvert G \rvert = \infty$ | Non-zero real numbers $(\mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}, \cdot)$ |
