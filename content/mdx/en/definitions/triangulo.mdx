<Capitular letra="A" /> triangle is the simplest <ConceptLink targetId="poligono">polygon</ConceptLink> in Euclidean planar geometry, forming the structural foundation of trigonometry and rigidity criteria. Formed by connecting three non-collinear <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink>, it bounds a convex region in the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="">
  Given three non-collinear <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>points</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="vertice-a" color="musgo">$A$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="vertice-b" color="musgo">$B$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="vertice-c" color="musgo">$C$</VisualBind> in a plane $\mathcal{P}$, the triangle <VisualBind element="triangulo" color="musgo">$\triangle ABC$</VisualBind> is the planar figure defined by the union of the three <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>line segments</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="lado-ab" color="pavo">$\overline{AB}$</VisualBind>, <VisualBind element="lado-bc" color="terracota">$\overline{BC}$</VisualBind>, and <VisualBind element="lado-ca" color="ocre">$\overline{CA}$</VisualBind>:
  $$
  \triangle ABC = \overline{AB} \cup \overline{BC} \cup \overline{CA}
  $$
  The points $A, B, C$ are the <ConceptLink targetId="vertice">vertices</ConceptLink> and the segments $\overline{AB}, \overline{BC}, \overline{CA}$ are the sides of the triangle.
</Definicion>

### Metric and angular classification

Triangles are classified according to the <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> relations among their sides and the measures of their interior <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink>:

| Classification by sides | Geometric condition | Classification by angles | Angular condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equilateral | $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{BC} \cong \overline{CA}$ | Acute | Three <ConceptLink targetId="angulo-agudo">acute</ConceptLink> angles |
| Isosceles | At least two congruent sides | <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo-rectangulo">Right</ConceptLink> | One <ConceptLink targetId="angulo-recto">right</ConceptLink> angle |
| Scalene | No congruent side pairs | Obtuse | One <ConceptLink targetId="angulo-obtuso">obtuse</ConceptLink> angle |
