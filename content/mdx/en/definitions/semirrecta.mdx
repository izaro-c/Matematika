<Capitular letra="A" /> ray (or half-line) is a continuous one-dimensional portion of a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> starting from a fixed origin and extending without bound in a single direction. It constitutes an indispensable geometric construct for defining <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> and setting directions in the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="">
  Given an origin point <VisualBind element="pA" color="musgo">$O$</VisualBind> and a distinct point $A$ on a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="lineOA" color="carbon">$\ell$</VisualBind>, the closed ray <VisualBind element="rayOA" color="musgo">$\overrightarrow{OA}$</VisualBind> is the set comprising the <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>point</ConceptLink> $O$, the point $A$, and all points $P \in \ell$ such that $O$ is not <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> $A$ and $P$:
  $$
  \overrightarrow{OA} = \{ P \in \ell \mid P = O \lor \neg(A * O * P) \}
  $$
  In terms of affine combinations on the vector space $\mathbb{R}^n$:
  $$
  \overrightarrow{OA} = \{ P \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid P = O + t(A - O), \, t \ge 0 \}
  $$
</Definicion>

### Synthetic geometry

In Hilbert's axiomatic system:
- **Line separation**: Every point $O \in \ell$ partitions the line $\ell$ into two disjoint opposite rays with common origin $O$.
- **Opposite rays**: If $A * O * B$, the rays $\overrightarrow{OA}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB}$ are opposite and satisfy $\overrightarrow{OA} \cap \overrightarrow{OB} = \{O\}$ and $\overrightarrow{OA} \cup \overrightarrow{OB} = \ell$.

### Analytic geometry

Given a unit direction vector $\vec{u} = \frac{A - O}{\|A - O\|}$, the ray is parameterized as:
$$
\vec{r}(t) = \vec{x}_O + t\vec{u}, \quad t \in [0, +\infty)
$$
