<Capitular letra="A" /> ray (or half-line) is the <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto">set</ConceptLink> consisting of a <ConceptLink targetId="punto" isDependency={true}>point</ConceptLink> fixed as an origin on a <ConceptLink targetId="recta" isDependency={true}>line</ConceptLink> and all points of that line extending unboundedly from it in a single <ConceptLink targetId="sentido">sense</ConceptLink>.

In the axiomatic foundation of <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink>, the ray is a derived concept formally built from order relations (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-1" isDependency={true}>Axiom II.1</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-orden-2" isDependency={true}>Axiom II.2</ConceptLink>). It constitutes the fundamental building block for defining <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> and setting <ConceptLink targetId="orientacion">orientations</ConceptLink> in the <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal Definition of Ray">
Given an origin point <VisualBind element="pB" color="carbon">$O$</VisualBind> and a distinct point <VisualBind element="pA" color="terracota">$A$</VisualBind> in the primitive <ConceptLink targetId="dominio">domain</ConceptLink> $\mathcal{P}$ <ConceptLink targetId="incidencia">incident</ConceptLink> with a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> <VisualBind element="lineOA" color="mora">$\ell$</VisualBind>, the **open ray** $\operatorname{sem}(OA)$ is the set of all points $P \in \mathcal{P}$ incident with $\ell$ such that $P \neq O$ and $O$ is not <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>between</ConceptLink> $A$ and $P$:

$$
\operatorname{sem}(OA) = \{ P \in \mathcal{P} \mid P \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land P \neq O \land \neg(A * O * P) \}
$$

The **closed ray** <VisualBind element="rayOA" color="musgo">$\overrightarrow{OA}$</VisualBind> is the union of the open ray with the origin point $O$:

$$
\overrightarrow{OA} = \operatorname{sem}(OA) \cup \{O\} = \{ P \in \mathcal{P} \mid P \, \mathbf{I} \, \ell \land (P = O \lor P = A \lor O * P * A \lor O * A * P) \}
$$
</Definicion>

  <PropiedadesGrupo title="Structural and Order Properties">
    <PropiedadItem 
      id="particion-recta" 
      title="Partition of a Line into Opposite Rays"
      formula="\operatorname{tr}(\ell) \setminus \{O\} = \operatorname{sem}(OA) \sqcup \operatorname{sem}(OB) \quad (A * O * B)"
    >
      Any point $O \in \mathcal{P}$ on a line $\ell$ partitions the set of remaining points $\operatorname{tr}(\ell) \setminus \{O\}$ into two open, <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-disjunto">disjoint</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="semirrectas-opuestas">opposite rays</ConceptLink>, $\operatorname{sem}(OA)$ and $\operatorname{sem}(OB)$, such that $A * O * B$.
    </PropiedadItem>

    <PropiedadItem 
      id="convexidad-semirrecta" 
      title="Linear Convexity"
      formula="P, Q \in \overrightarrow{OA} \land P * X * Q \implies X \in \overrightarrow{OA}"
    >
      Every closed ray $\overrightarrow{OA}$ is a linear <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto-convexo">convex set</ConceptLink>: if $P, Q \in \overrightarrow{OA}$, then for any point $X \in \mathcal{P}$ satisfying the betweenness relation $P * X * Q$, it necessarily holds that $X \in \overrightarrow{OA}$.
    </PropiedadItem>

    <PropiedadItem 
      id="orden-lineal-semirrecta" 
      title="Induced Linear Order with Minimum Element"
      formula="(\overrightarrow{OA}, \le) \implies \min(\overrightarrow{OA}) = O"
    >
      The betweenness relation on the closed ray $\overrightarrow{OA}$ induces a strict <ConceptLink targetId="orden-total">linear order</ConceptLink> ($\le$) on its points ($P \le Q \iff P = O \lor P = Q \lor O * P * Q$), where the origin $O$ serves as the unique <ConceptLink targetId="elemento-minimo">minimum element</ConceptLink>.
    </PropiedadItem>
  </PropiedadesGrupo>
</SeccionPropiedades>
