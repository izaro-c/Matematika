<Capitular letra="A" /> homogeneous binary relation is **transitive** if the relational link necessarily propagates through chained intermediate elements.

It constitutes the formal property that permits the transfer of equalities, equivalences, and orderings, ensuring that indirect comparisons retain the same logical validity as direct ones.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of transitive relation">
  Let $A$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto" isDependency={true}>set</ConceptLink> and let $R \subseteq A \times A$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="relacion-binaria">binary relation</ConceptLink> on $A$. The relation $R$ is **transitive** if and only if:
  $$
  \forall x, y, z \in A, \quad (x \, R \, y \land y \, R \, z) \implies x \, R \, z
  $$
  In terms of relational composition, denoting by $R \circ R = \{(x, z) \mid \exists y \in A \ (x \, R \, y \land y \, R \, z)\}$ the composition of $R$ with itself, transitivity is equivalent to the inclusion:
  $$
  R \circ R \subseteq R
  $$
</Definicion>

### Fundamental role in congruence and parallelism

Transitivity serves as a cornerstone in fundamental geometric deductions:

- **Postulation in congruence:** In synthetic foundations, the transitivity of segment <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> ($\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD} \land \overline{CD} \cong \overline{EF} \implies \overline{AB} \cong \overline{EF}$) is neither self-evident nor deducible from reflexivity: it must be expressly postulated in <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-2">Axiom III.2</ConceptLink>.
- **Transitivity of parallelism:** In a single plane, two lines parallel to a third line are parallel to each other ($\ell \parallel m \land m \parallel n \implies \ell \parallel n$). This result is a pivotal <ConceptLink targetId="teorema">theorem</ConceptLink> derived from Playfair's parallel postulate (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides">Axiom IV</ConceptLink>), conferring upon Euclidean <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallelism</ConceptLink> the full status of an <ConceptLink targetId="relacion-equivalencia">equivalence relation</ConceptLink>.
- **Order structures:** In ordered sets, transitivity ensures the coherence of inequality chains ($x < y \land y < z \implies x < z$).
