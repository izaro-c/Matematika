<Capitular letra="I" />n mathematical logic and model theory, a **domain** (or universe of discourse) is the non-empty <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto">set</ConceptLink> of objects upon which the relations and functions of a <ConceptLink targetId="estructura-geometrica">mathematical structure</ConceptLink> operate.

In many-sorted theories (such as <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-sintetica">synthetic geometry</ConceptLink>), several distinct and disjoint domains coexist, such as the domain of <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink> ($\mathcal{P}$), the domain of <ConceptLink targetId="recta">lines</ConceptLink> ($\mathcal{L}$), and the domain of <ConceptLink targetId="plano">planes</ConceptLink> ($\Pi$).
