<Capitular letra="A" /> **polygonal region** is the <ConceptLink targetId="conjunto">set</ConceptLink> of points of a <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink> that can be expressed as a finite union of <ConceptLink targetId="region-triangular" isDependency={true}>triangular regions</ConceptLink> whose interiors are pairwise disjoint.

Every polygonal region admits multiple distinct triangular decompositions, but its spatial extent is unique. The class of all polygonal regions of a plane constitutes the canonical domain upon which the <ConceptLink targetId="teoria-medida-area">theory of area measure</ConceptLink> is formulated and the notions of <ConceptLink targetId="equidescomponibilidad">equidecomposability</ConceptLink> are established.
