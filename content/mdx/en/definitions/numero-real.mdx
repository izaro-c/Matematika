<Capitular letra="T" />he **real numbers** constitute the <ConceptLink targetId="continuo">numerical continuum</ConceptLink> formalized as the unique complete <ConceptLink targetId="cuerpo-ordenado">ordered field</ConceptLink>, extending the <ConceptLink targetId="numero-racional">rational numbers</ConceptLink> through the absorption of <ConceptLink targetId="inconmensurabilidad">incommensurable</ConceptLink> magnitudes or <ConceptLink targetId="numero-irracional">irrational numbers</ConceptLink>.

Constructed by <ConceptLink targetId="dedekind">Richard Dedekind</ConceptLink> via <ConceptLink targetId="cortadura-dedekind">cuts</ConceptLink> on $\mathbb{Q}$ and by <ConceptLink targetId="cantor">Georg Cantor</ConceptLink> through <ConceptLink targetId="clase-equivalencia">equivalence classes</ConceptLink> of <ConceptLink targetId="sucesion-cauchy">Cauchy sequences</ConceptLink>, the real numbers fill every gap of the <ConceptLink targetId="recta">number line</ConceptLink>, grounding <ConceptLink targetId="limite">limits</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="calculo-infinitesimal">infinitesimal calculus</ConceptLink>, and <ConceptLink targetId="geometria-analitica">analytic geometry</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal Structure of the Real Numbers">
  The system of **real numbers** is a structure $(\mathbb{R}, +, \cdot, \le)$ that constitutes a commutative <ConceptLink targetId="cuerpo" isDependency={true}>field</ConceptLink> endowed with a compatible <ConceptLink targetId="orden-total">total order</ConceptLink> relation extending the field of <ConceptLink targetId="numero-racional" isDependency={true}>rational numbers</ConceptLink> $\mathbb{Q}$ and satisfying the <ConceptLink targetId="completitud-reales" isDependency={true}>completeness of the real numbers</ConceptLink>:
  $$
  \forall S \subseteq \mathbb{R} \ (S \neq \emptyset \land \exists M \in \mathbb{R} \ \forall x \in S \ (x \le M)) \implies \exists s \in \mathbb{R} \ (s = \sup(S))
  $$
  This property ensures the analytic <ConceptLink targetId="continuidad" isDependency={true}>continuity</ConceptLink> of the real line, deducing that every sequence of nested <ConceptLink targetId="intervalo">closed intervals</ConceptLink> $[a_n, b_n]$ with $a_n \le a_{n+1} \le b_{n+1} \le b_n$ possesses a non-empty intersection:
  $$
  \bigcap_{n=1}^\infty [a_n, b_n] \neq \emptyset
  $$
</Definicion>
