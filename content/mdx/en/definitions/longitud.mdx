<Capitular letra="L" />**ength** is a one-dimensional metric measure function that assigns to each <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>segment</ConceptLink> a positive <ConceptLink targetId="numero-real">real number</ConceptLink>, invariantly under <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruence</ConceptLink> and additively with respect to the relation of <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>betweenness</ConceptLink>.

Unlike purely qualitative comparisons based on the synthetic congruence relation $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}$, assigning a numerical scalar magnitude $|\cdot|: \mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{R}^+$ requires fixing a reference <ConceptLink targetId="segmento-unidad" isDependency={true}>unit segment</ConceptLink> and relying on Hilbert's continuity axioms: <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-arquimedes" isDependency={true}>Axiom V.1 (Archimedes)</ConceptLink> to ensure that no non-zero geometric magnitude is infinitesimal with respect to another, and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-completitud" isDependency={true}>Axiom V.2 (Line completeness)</ConceptLink> to guarantee an isomorphism with the continuous field of real numbers $\mathbb{R}$. This notion underpins Euclidean <ConceptLink targetId="distancia">distance</ConceptLink> and bridges the synthetic structure of the <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> with real analysis.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of the length function">
Let $\mathcal{S} = \{ \overline{AB} \subset \mathcal{P} : A, B \in \mathcal{P}, A \neq B \}$ be the set of all non-degenerate <ConceptLink targetId="segmento" isDependency={true}>segments</ConceptLink> in geometric space determined by pairs of <ConceptLink targetId="punto">points</ConceptLink>. Fixing a reference segment $u_0 \in \mathcal{S}$ termed the **unit of length**, the **length function** is the unique map

$$
|\cdot|: \mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{R}^+
$$

satisfying the following constitutive postulates:

1. **Unit normalization:** The unit segment has length one:
$$
|u_0| = 1
$$

2. **Invariance under congruence:** Two segments have the same length if and only if they are <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia" isDependency={true}>congruent</ConceptLink>:
$$
|AB| = |CD| \iff \overline{AB} \cong \overline{CD}
$$

3. **Additivity under betweenness:** If three points $A, B, C \in \mathcal{P}$ satisfy the relation $A * B * C$ (point $B$ <ConceptLink targetId="estar-entre" isDependency={true}>is between</ConceptLink> $A$ and $C$), the length of the joined segment is the sum of the lengths of its component parts:
$$
A * B * C \implies |AC| = |AB| + |BC|
$$

The existence and uniqueness of this real-valued measure function rests deductively upon <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-3" isDependency={true}>Axiom III.3</ConceptLink>, which makes additivity compatible with invariance under congruence, and upon <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-arquimedes" isDependency={true}>Axiom V.1</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-completitud" isDependency={true}>Axiom V.2</ConceptLink>.
</Definicion>

<SeccionPropiedades>
  <PropiedadesGrupo title="Metric properties of the length function">
    <PropiedadItem 
      id="positividad-estricta" 
      title="Strict positivity"
      formula="\forall \overline{AB} \in \mathcal{S}, \quad |AB| > 0"
    >
      Every non-degenerate segment has a strictly positive length. The function does not map any segment with distinct endpoints to zero in the metric space.
    </PropiedadItem>

    <PropiedadItem 
      id="desigualdad-triangular-longitud" 
      title="Metric triangle inequality"
      formula="|AC| \le |AB| + |BC|"
    >
      For any three points $A, B, C \in \mathcal{P}$, the direct length between two points does not exceed the sum of the intermediate lengths, with equality holding if and only if $B$ is between $A$ and $C$ ($A * B * C$) or if endpoints coincide.
    </PropiedadItem>
  </PropiedadesGrupo>

  <PropiedadesGrupo title="Deductive foundation and continuity">
    <PropiedadItem 
      id="isomorfismo-aditivo-real" 
      title="Isomorphism with the real additive semigroup"
      formula="(\mathcal{S}/{\cong}, +) \cong (\mathbb{R}^+, +)"
    >
      The quotient set of segment congruence classes, endowed with the addition induced by linear concatenation along a line, forms a continuous algebraic structure that is isomorphic to the additive semigroup of positive real numbers $(\mathbb{R}^+, +)$.
    </PropiedadItem>
  </PropiedadesGrupo>
</SeccionPropiedades>

<Nota>
The segment congruence relation and linear order are primitive and independent of the construction of the real number field. The length function is the metric arithmetization that arises upon appending the continuity axioms.
</Nota>
