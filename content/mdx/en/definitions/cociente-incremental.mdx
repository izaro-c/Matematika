<Capitular letra="T" />he <VisualBind element="secante" color="terracota">**difference quotient**</VisualBind> (or average rate of change) of a <ConceptLink targetId="numero-real">real</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="funcion" isDependency={true}>function</ConceptLink> between two points of an <ConceptLink targetId="intervalo" isDependency={true}>interval</ConceptLink> is the quotient of the change in the function by the change in its independent variable: it measures how much the function changes, on average, per unit change of the variable.

<Definicion title="Formal definition of difference quotient">
  Let $I \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be an <ConceptLink targetId="intervalo" isDependency={true}>interval</ConceptLink> of the <ConceptLink targetId="numero-real" isDependency={true}>real line</ConceptLink> and let $f\colon I \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a <VisualBind element="grafica" color="carbon"><ConceptLink targetId="funcion" isDependency={true}>function</ConceptLink></VisualBind>. Fixing a base point $x \in I$ and given a non-zero increment $h \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$ such that $x + h \in I$, the <VisualBind element="secante" color="terracota">**difference quotient**</VisualBind> of $f$ between <VisualBind element="pX" color="carbon">$x$</VisualBind> and <VisualBind element="pXh" color="terracota">$x + h$</VisualBind> is defined as:
  $$
  \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}
  $$
  Equivalently, setting $x_1 = x + h$ with $x_1 \neq x$, the quotient is expressed as:
  $$
  \frac{f(x_1) - f(x)}{x_1 - x}
  $$
  where <VisualBind element="segDy" color="pavo">$\Delta y = f(x + h) - f(x)$</VisualBind> represents the change in the dependent variable and <VisualBind element="segDx" color="ocre">$\Delta x = h$</VisualBind> represents the increment of the independent variable.
</Definicion>
