To compare triangles without relying on informal intuition, we apply the axioms of <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-5">congruence</ConceptLink> (SAS). ### Stage 3: Parallelism and angle sum

The Pythagorean theorem is exclusive to plane Euclidean geometry. This is because it depends fundamentally on <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides">Euclid's fifth postulate</ConceptLink>. Without this axiom, it is impossible to prove that the interior angles of a triangle sum to exactly $180^\circ$.
