<Capitular letra="T" />he Angle-Side-Angle (ASA) congruence theorem establishes one of the foundational criteria for geometric rigidity and metric equivalence between <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink> in planar geometry. It guarantees that fixing a <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segment</ConceptLink> and the inclinations of the two rays extending from its endpoints uniquely determines the triangle's geometry.

<Separador />

### Formal statement

Let $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle A'B'C'$ be two <VisualBind element="triangulos" color="musgo"><ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangles</ConceptLink></VisualBind> in the Euclidean plane. Suppose the following <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> relations hold:
1. The included side is congruent: $\overline{AB} \cong \overline{A'B'}$.
2. The adjacent angles to the segment are respectively congruent: $\angle CAB \cong \angle C'A'B'$ and $\angle ABC \cong \angle A'B'C'$.

Then, overall triangle congruence holds necessarily:

<Formula>
  $$ \triangle ABC \cong \triangle A'B'C' $$
</Formula>

Consequently, all corresponding homologous sides and the third angles are congruent: $\overline{BC} \cong \overline{B'C'}$, $\overline{CA} \cong \overline{C'A'}$, and $\angle BCA \cong \angle B'C'A'$.

<Separador />

### Proof and axiomatic foundations

In <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert's</ConceptLink> axiomatic framework, the SAS criterion is taken as an <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-5" isDependency={true}>axiom of congruence</ConceptLink>, while the ASA criterion is rigorously proven by <ConceptLink targetId="metodo-contradiccion" isDependency={true}>contradiction</ConceptLink>:
- If $\overline{AC}$ were not congruent to $\overline{A'C'}$, there would exist a point $D$ on the corresponding ray such that $\overline{AD} \cong \overline{A'C'}$.
- By the SAS axiom, $\triangle ABD \cong \triangle A'B'C'$, implying $\angle ABD \cong \angle A'B'C'$.
- This would contradict the uniqueness of angle transport (<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-4" isDependency={true}>Axiom III.4</ConceptLink>) by producing two distinct angles of the same measure on the same half-plane.

<Separador />

### Derived criteria

- **AAS Criterion (Angle-Angle-Side)**: Combined with the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-suma-angulos-triangulo">triangle angle sum theorem</ConceptLink>, the criterion extends to any two angles and a non-included side.
- **Geodetic triangulation**: Forms the mathematical underpinning of triangulation and surveying to compute inaccessible distances.
