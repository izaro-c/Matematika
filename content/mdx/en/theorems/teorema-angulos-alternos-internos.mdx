<Capitular letra="T" />he alternate interior angles theorem establishes metric equivalence between <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> situated on opposite sides of a transversal <ConceptLink targetId="recta">secant line</ConceptLink> intersecting two <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel lines</ConceptLink> in the Euclidean <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Formal statement

Let $r$ and $s$ be two <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel lines</ConceptLink> in plane $\mathcal{P}$ ($r \parallel s$), and let $t$ be a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">transversal line</ConceptLink> intersecting them at points $A$ and $B$ respectively. Let $\alpha$ and $\alpha'$ be two interior angles lying between $r$ and $s$ on opposite half-planes relative to transversal $t$. Global <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink> holds necessarily:

<Formula>
  $$ \alpha \cong \alpha' $$
</Formula>

That is, the angular measures of both alternate interior angles are strictly identical: $|\alpha| = |\alpha'|$.

<Separador />

### Axiomatic analysis and converses

Euclid structures the angular properties generated by a transversal into two main Propositions:
- **Proposition I.27 (Absolute converse)**: If two coplanar lines cut by a transversal form congruent alternate interior angles, then the lines are <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel</ConceptLink>. This result belongs to absolute geometry and does not require the parallel postulate.
- **Proposition I.29 (Euclidean direct theorem)**: If two lines are <ConceptLink targetId="paralelas">parallel</ConceptLink>, the congruence of alternate interior angles indispensably requires <ConceptLink targetId="euclides">Euclid's</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides" isDependency={true}>parallel postulate</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Derived angular relations

Given two parallel lines intersected by transversal $t$:
- **Corresponding angles**: Are congruent ($\alpha \cong \beta$).
- **Alternate exterior angles**: Are congruent ($\gamma \cong \gamma'$).
- **Consecutive interior angles**: Are <ConceptLink targetId="angulos-suplementarios">supplementary</ConceptLink> ($\alpha + \beta' = 180^\circ$).
