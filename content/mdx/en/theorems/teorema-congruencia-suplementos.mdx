<Capitular letra="I" />f two <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> are <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruent</ConceptLink> to each other, then the <ConceptLink targetId="angulos-suplementarios" isDependency={true}>adjacent supplementary angles</ConceptLink> to each of them are also mutually congruent.

This result of <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink> was presented by <ConceptLink targetId="euclides">Euclid</ConceptLink> in Propositions I.13 and I.14 of the <ConceptLink targetId="elementos-euclides">Elements</ConceptLink> and formalized by <ConceptLink targetId="hilbert">David Hilbert</ConceptLink> in §6 of the *Grundlagen der Geometrie*. It establishes that angle congruence is preserved under taking supplements without invoking the <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides">parallel axiom</ConceptLink> or <ConceptLink targetId="medida-angular">angle measure</ConceptLink>. Its synthetic proof relies on the addition of <ConceptLink targetId="segmento">segments</ConceptLink> from <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-congruencia-3" isDependency={true}>Axiom III.3</ConceptLink> and on the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-congruencia-lal" isDependency={true}>SAS congruence theorem</ConceptLink>.

<Definicion title="Formal statement of the supplement congruence theorem">
Let $\angle(h, k)$ and $\angle(h', k')$ be two <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> such that:

$$
\angle(h, k) \cong \angle(h', k')
$$

where $h$ and $k$ are <ConceptLink targetId="semirrecta">rays</ConceptLink> originating at $O \in \mathcal{P}$, and $h'$ and $k'$ are rays originating at $O' \in \mathcal{P}$.

If $h_1$ is the ray opposite to $h$, and $h'_1$ is the ray opposite to $h'$ (<ConceptLink targetId="semirrectas-opuestas">opposite rays</ConceptLink>), determining the <ConceptLink targetId="angulos-suplementarios" isDependency={true}>adjacent supplementary angles</ConceptLink> $\angle(h_1, k)$ and $\angle(h'_1, k')$, then:

$$
\angle(h_1, k) \cong \angle(h'_1, k')
$$
</Definicion>
