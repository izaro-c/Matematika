<Capitular letra="T" />he hyperbolic geometry system —formulated independently by Nikolai Lobachevsky and János Bolyai— constitutes the first non-Euclidean geometry formally discovered. It is obtained by replacing the Euclidean parallel postulate with the hyperbolic parallel axiom within the framework of <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>, introducing a metric of constant negative Gaussian curvature.

<Separador />

### Axiomatic structure

The hyperbolic system integrates the full abstract formal framework:

1. **Foundation of <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-absoluto">absolute geometry</ConceptLink>**: Incorporates all axioms of <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-incidencia">incidence</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="orden">order</ConceptLink>, <ConceptLink targetId="congruencia">congruence</ConceptLink>, and continuity.
2. **<ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-hiperbolico" isDependency={true}>Hyperbolic parallel axiom</ConceptLink>**: Given a <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> $l$ and an external <ConceptLink targetId="punto">point</ConceptLink> $P \notin l$ in a <ConceptLink targetId="plano">plane</ConceptLink>, there exist at least two distinct lines $l_1, l_2$ containing $P$ that do not intersect $l$:
   $$ \forall l \in \mathcal{L}, \forall P \notin l, \exists l_1, l_2 \in \mathcal{L} \text{ such that } l_1 \neq l_2, P \in l_1 \cap l_2 \land l_1 \cap l = \emptyset \land l_2 \cap l = \emptyset $$

<Separador />

### Characteristic theorems

The negative curvature metric fundamentally alters spatial metric and triangular properties:

- **Angular defect**: The sum of interior <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> in any hyperbolic <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink> is strictly less than $180^\circ$:
  $$ \sum_{i=1}^3 \alpha_i < 180^\circ $$
- **Proportionality of <ConceptLink targetId="area">area</ConceptLink>**: The area $\text{Area}(\triangle)$ of a triangle is directly proportional to its angular defect $\delta(\triangle) = \pi - (\alpha + \beta + \gamma)$.
- **Non-existence of rectangles and non-congruent similarities**: There exist no quadrilaterals with four right angles. Two triangles sharing identical corresponding angles are necessarily congruent (AAA criterion).

### Models and consistency

- **<ConceptLink targetId="modelo-poincare">Poincaré disk $\mathbb{D}^2$</ConceptLink>**: Canonical conformal model where hyperbolic lines are circular arcs orthogonal to the boundary.
- **Relative consistency**: The existence of analytic conformal models in the complex plane reduces the logical consistency of hyperbolic geometry to standard real analysis.
