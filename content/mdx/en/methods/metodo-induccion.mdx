<Capitular letra="M" />athematical induction is a fundamental deductive proof technique used to establish the universal validity of an infinite family of statements $\{P(n)\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ indexed over the set of natural numbers $\mathbb{N}$. Axiomatically, it is rooted in the well-ordering principle of $\mathbb{N}$ and Peano's axioms.

<Separador />

### Structure of an Inductive Proof

A proof by mathematical induction consists of two mandatory canonical stages:

1. **Base Case**: Demonstrating directly that the statement holds for the initial element (typically $n = 1$ or $n = 0$):
   <Formula>
     $$ P(1) \text{ is true} $$
   </Formula>

2. **Inductive Step**: Assuming as the *induction hypothesis* that the statement holds for an arbitrary positive integer $k \ge 1$ ($P(k)$), and rigorously deriving that it must also hold for the successor $k + 1$:
   <Formula>
     $$ \forall k \in \mathbb{N}, \quad P(k) \implies P(k+1) $$
   </Formula>

Once both conditions are verified, the principle of mathematical induction establishes universal truth across all natural numbers:

<Formula>
  $$ [P(1) \land (\forall k \ge 1, \, P(k) \implies P(k+1))] \implies \forall n \in \mathbb{N}, \, P(n) $$
</Formula>

<Separador />

### Essential Applications

- **Finite Sums and Sequences**: Proving closed-form summations such as the sum of the first $n$ positive integers:
  $$ \sum_{i=1}^n i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$
- **Combinatorial Geometry**: Proving that the sum of the interior <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> of a simple $n$-sided polygon is $(n-2) \times 180^\circ$, generalizing the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-suma-angulos-triangulo">triangle angle sum theorem</ConceptLink> via successive triangulations.
- **Inequalities and Divisibility**: Validating classical bounds like Bernoulli's inequality $(1+x)^n \ge 1+nx$ for $x > -1$.

<Separador />

### Formal Variations

- **Strong (Complete) Induction**: The induction hypothesis assumes that $P(m)$ holds for all integers $1 \le m \le k$ in order to deduce $P(k+1)$.
- **Structural and Transfinite Induction**: Generalizations of the induction principle applied to recursively defined data structures and transfinite ordinals in axiomatic set theory.
