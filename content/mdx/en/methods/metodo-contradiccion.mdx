<Capitular letra="P" />roof by contradiction, historically known as *reductio ad absurdum*, is an indirect proof technique that establishes the truth of a statement $P$ by provisionally assuming its negation $\neg P$ and rigorously deriving a formal contradiction of the form $R \land \neg R \equiv \bot$.

<Separador />

### Logical Foundations and the Excluded Middle

The method relies strictly on the foundational principles of classical logic:

- **Law of Non-Contradiction**: A proposition cannot be simultaneously true and false within the same formal framework: $\neg (R \land \neg R)$.
- **Law of Excluded Middle**: Every well-formed statement $P$ is either true or false: $P \lor \neg P$.
- **Implication Equivalence**: Demonstrating that $(\neg P \implies \bot)$ is logically equivalent to establishing the truth of $P$:
  <Formula>
    $$ (\neg P \implies \bot) \iff P $$
  </Formula>

For a conditional implication $P \implies Q$, the method assumes both the hypothesis $P$ and the negation of the conclusion $\neg Q$, subsequently deriving an inconsistency with the axiomatic foundations or established theorems.

<Separador />

### Classical Geometric and Analytic Examples

- **Irrationality of $\sqrt{2}$**: Assuming $\sqrt{2} = a/b$ with $\gcd(a, b) = 1$ leads deductively to the conclusion that both $a$ and $b$ are even, contradicting the coprimality hypothesis.
- **Uniqueness of Perpendiculars**: To prove that at most one <ConceptLink targetId="perpendicular">perpendicular line</ConceptLink> passes through a point external to a given <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink>, one assumes two distinct perpendiculars exist. This would form a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink> with two right <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink>, contradicting the <ConceptLink targetId="teorema-suma-angulos-triangulo">triangle angle sum theorem</ConceptLink>.

<Separador />

### Methodological Features

- **Essential for Non-Existence and Uniqueness**: The primary strategy for establishing that certain mathematical configurations or counterexamples cannot exist.
- **Non-Constructive Nature**: It proves that an object exists or a relation holds without necessarily constructing the witness object explicitly.
