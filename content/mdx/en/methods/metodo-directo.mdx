<Capitular letra="T" />he direct proof method is the primary deductive strategy in mathematics for establishing the validity of a conditional statement $P \implies Q$. Its procedure formally assumes the truth of the <ConceptLink targetId="hipotesis">hypothesis</ConceptLink> $P$ and constructs a finite sequence of rigorous logical inferences to necessarily conclude the truth of the <ConceptLink targetId="tesis">thesis</ConceptLink> $Q$.

<Separador />

### Logical structure

The deductive argument chains intermediate propositions through the inference rule *modus ponens*:

<Formula>
  $$ P \implies P_1 \implies P_2 \implies \dots \implies P_n \implies Q $$
</Formula>

Each step in the deductive chain is strictly justified by:

- The <ConceptLink targetId="axioma">axioms</ConceptLink> of the corresponding <ConceptLink targetId="sistema-axiomatico">formal system</ConceptLink>.
- Previously proved <ConceptLink targetId="teorema">theorems</ConceptLink> or lemmas.
- Formal definitions of the underlying objects.
- Universal laws of propositional and predicate logic.

<Separador />

### Notable examples

- **<ConceptLink targetId="teorema-suma-angulos-triangulo">Sum of angles of a triangle</ConceptLink>**: Starting from the definition of a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo">triangle</ConceptLink> and the <ConceptLink targetId="axioma-paralelas-euclides">parallel axiom</ConceptLink>, an auxiliary <ConceptLink targetId="recta">line</ConceptLink> <ConceptLink targetId="paralelismo">parallel</ConceptLink> to the base is drawn through the opposite <ConceptLink targetId="vertice">vertex</ConceptLink> to directly derive that its interior <ConceptLink targetId="angulo">angles</ConceptLink> sum to $180^\circ$.
- **<ConceptLink targetId="teorema-pitagoras">Pythagorean theorem</ConceptLink>**: By geometric decomposition of the <ConceptLink targetId="area">area</ConceptLink> of squares erected on the <ConceptLink targetId="cateto">legs</ConceptLink> and <ConceptLink targetId="hipotenusa">hypotenuse</ConceptLink> of a <ConceptLink targetId="triangulo-rectangulo">right triangle</ConceptLink>, the algebraic identity $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ is directly concluded.

<Separador />

### Properties and applicability

- **Constructivity**: Provides an explicit deductive path linking the initial premise directly to the final conclusion.
- **Deductive transparency**: Every intermediate step is individually verifiable.
- **Scope**: Serves as the preferred approach whenever the hypothesis provides explicit relations that can be directly manipulated without requiring auxiliary hypotheses from <ConceptLink targetId="metodo-contradiccion">proof by contradiction</ConceptLink>.
